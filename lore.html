The world of the six Orders, seven moons and one Wanderer.

The Pawtheon Codex

Explore the current lore of Pawtheon: its histories, beliefs, creatures and living lunar bonds. The world stands around 1,200 YFH — years since the First Howl.

The world at a glance

  • 85 continuing lunar seats: Flame 18, Radiant 12, Deep 13, Wild 18, Arcane 13 and Dream 11. The Wanderer is the unaffiliated 86th Wizard. A vacated bond may choose a successor.
  • A civil year has 13 months of 28 days. Physical moon phases drift through that calendar. A true seven-moon Convergence returns every 495 years: last 1196 YFH, next 1691.
  • The Great Silence lasted 49 hours in 1100 YFH. The Loyalty Wars ran from 1145 to 1150; the Accords followed in 1155. Recorded deaths: 264 Order-affiliated personnel and 10,300 civilians, not 264 active Wizard seats.
  • The Starter Lands lack the ground Current. Hat-focused direct lunar channels still permit training; a new moon silences its own channel, not ordinary dreams or plant growth.

Chapter I and II retain their confirmed text. The chronology companion explains early focus structures, later Order Spires and the Archivum’s older sacred walls. Codex folios are revised 1200 YFH editions; attributed doctrines remain in-world beliefs.
