YOUR WIZARD JOURNEY

Quests & experience

Level 1 Wizard

Start at level 1. Every new Crystal Lizard earns 25 XP, Moon Shard 20 XP, landmark story 15 XP, and wizard met 10 XP. Every Crystal Lizard Scale pickup earns 10 XP, including repeat pickups. Lizards and Moon Shards respawn after five minutes and stack without a gameplay inventory cap. Complete quests below for extra XP.

Discoveries train your active Order. Each Order reaches level 50 separately; their mastery grows your overall wizard level. Repeatable surveys in the Spellbook earn 800 XP per route. Previous collections count.

A WORLD TO WALK THROUGH

Your first steps

Use WASD or arrow keys to walk. Hold Shift to run. Drag the scene to turn the camera, and scroll to zoom. Space jumps. Goldforge (#8667) can toggle Solar Flight with G or the flight button. Steer normally in flight; Space or Rise climbs, Ctrl or Descend lowers altitude, and G lands. H howls. F casts your first equipped Order spell; Q unlocks as a second slot at Order level 20, and V is the third slot at level 50. On touch screens, tap the corresponding spell button. Press K or tap Spellbook to browse every Order’s magic, effects, unlock levels, upgrades and repeatable surveys. Spells recharge after use. Approach a Wisdom Owl and press E or tap its prompt to read its lore and receive directions to an uncollected lizard or Moon Shard in its realm. E collects a nearby crystal lizard, meets a nearby dog, inspects a relic display, or listens at a landmark. P opens the character picker; C opens your inventory.

On touch screens, use the left thumbstick to move and drag on the right to look. Double-tap the joystick to keep walking in your last direction; touch it again or tap Stop walking to stop. Tap Sprint to toggle running. Use a second finger to interact, cast, or howl while moving. Reading a story or opening a menu stops auto-walk. Menu holds your dogs, inventory, quests, mini-map, and settings.

The mini-map follows you with north at the top. Use + and − to zoom, tap the map to open the Atlas, or Hide to collapse it. Open the Atlas to visit every realm. The other dogs walk within their native realms. The Wanderer travels through all seven realms and the Starter Lands when you play as another dog. Approach one to hear their story and advance the Meet Every Wizard quest. This is an expandable exploration prototype; combat and detailed character art can be added next. Order spells currently support exploration; they do not damage dogs or creatures.
