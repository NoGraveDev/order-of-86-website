The 86 Wizards

  • 85 Wizards in 6 Orders (counts fixed: Flame 18, Radiant 12, Deep 13, Wild 18, Arcane 13, Dream 11)
  • 1 Wanderer (The Gray Dog, Dog #6164, rank 7277, gray Classic, round glasses, pointy purple hat)
  • The Wanderer is NOT counted in any Order. His hat resonates with all six + Palehowl.
  • Magic is borrowed from moons, distributed equally among Order members. Order size influences lunar rhythm; cycle length is not a numerical headcount law. Umbra has a fourteen-day cycle and thirteen Arcane seats; the Wanderer is a separate purple-hat channel, not a fourteenth Arcane member.
  • Full moon = magic surges beyond control. New moon = no magic, only self.
  • A hat is a channel, not a symbol. Without it, a dog feels the Current but cannot shape it.

Key Events

  • The First Howl: The Gray Dog howled; seven moons woke; magic poured into the world; dogs evolved over generations.
  • The Second Howl: Hats fell from six moons (85 total); the Gray Dog received the last hat (curved brim, purple); Palehowl created the Starter Lands.
  • The Great Silence: All magic died for 49 hours. Cause unknown to most in-world witnesses; author-level continuity identifies the Hollow's grasp.
  • The Loyalty Wars: Post-tension conflicts between Orders/realms.
  • The Great Rot: Corruption in Shadowmire; Zombie Wizards are death-touched but not necessarily evil.
  • The Starter Lands: Neutral ground at world's center; the ground Current does not run there; hat-focused direct lunar channels still function, allowing supervised training.
  • The Lunar Convergence: When all seven moons align. During this, Palehowl blazes brighter than all six Order Moons combined.

Systems

  • The Current: Raw magical energy network running through the world.
  • The Loyalty Network: Mystical connection between all Wizards; shivers when major events occur.
  • Dream-threads: Oneiroic bonds between Wizards; Dream Order amplifies them.

NEW CANON ELEMENTS (Established by This Build)

The Cosmic Antagonist

Name: The Hollow Nature: The void that existed before the First Howl. Not evil but hungry. It is the silence that predates sound, and it wants the moons extinguished so the universe can return to quiet. Relationship to Gray Dog: The Gray Dog came from between — from the edge of the Hollow. He stole sound from the silence. The Hollow has been reaching back ever since. Manifestation: The Great Silence was the Hollow's first successful grasp. It lasted 49 hours. The Hollow is patient; it will try again.

The Anti-Magic Faction

Name: The Mute Core Belief: The moons are slavemasters. The hats are chains. True devotion must be earned, not poured down from the sky. The First Howl was not a gift; it was an invasion. Founder: A former Flame Wizard named Ashpaw who renounced his hat during the Great Silence. Doctrine: "A dog who needs a hat to be loyal is not loyal at all." Symbols: A broken hat brim. A closed mouth (no howling). Methods: Sabotage of moon-cycle rituals. Recruitment of dogs who lost magic during the Great Silence and never got it back.

Pre-Howl Spirituality

Name: The Old Pack Ways Core Belief: The moons were Silent Gods — beautiful, distant, watching but never speaking. Dogs believed the Silent Gods would answer if a pack howled in perfect unison for seven nights. Shamans: Called Listeners — dogs with unusually sharp hearing who claimed to hear whispers from the Silent Gods. Afterlife: The Long Run — an endless plain where packs chase the horizon forever, never tiring, never hungry. Rituals: The Circle Howl (packs howled in rotation, each dog taking a note), Bone-keeping (preserving the skulls of alpha dogs for guidance), Scent-trails to the dead (leaving a dead packmate's favorite trail for their spirit to follow).

The Liturgical Language

Name: Urfic (from "urf" — primal howl) Status: Dead language; preserved only in sacred texts and Tower inscriptions. Characteristics: Built on vowel-tones corresponding to the seven moons. No written alphabet — only howl-notation (pitch and duration marks). Sacred Vowels:

  • A = Emberhowl (courage)
  • O = Solaris (truth)
  • U = Deepwell (depth)
  • E = Evergreen (growth)
  • I = Umbra (hidden)
  • AE = Roseglow (dream)
  • Y = Palehowl (possibility)

Editorial Conventions for In-World Texts

  • [Howled] — oral tradition transcribed from pack memory
  • [Trans. from Urfic] — translation from liturgical language
  • [Doubtful] — passage authenticity questioned by scholars
  • [Pack Memory] — preserved through the Loyalty Network, not written
  • [Ed. Note] — commentary by in-world editors
  • [Pre-Howl] — source predates the First Howl (rare, usually archaeological)

Dating System

  • YFH — Years Since the First Howl
  • PH — Pre-Howl (archaeological layer only)
  • Current in-universe "present" is approximately 1,200 YFH
  • The Second Howl occurred at approximately 400 YFH
  • The Great Silence occurred at approximately 1,100 YFH
  • The Loyalty Wars occurred at 1,145–1,150 YFH; the Accords were signed in 1,155 YFH

Calendar

  • Year: 364 days (52 weeks of 7 days)
  • Days of the week: named after the moons' sacred vowels

- A-day (Emberhowl's day) - O-day (Solaris's day) - U-day (Deepwell's day) - E-day (Evergreen's day) - I-day (Umbra's day) - AE-day (Roseglow's day) - Y-day (Palehowl's day) — rest day, day of possibility

  • Months: 13 civil months of 28 days each (symbolically lunar, not physical phase cycles)
  • Leap: No leap year; the civil year is fixed; physical lunar phases drift against it
