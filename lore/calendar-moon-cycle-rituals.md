## What Happens at New Moon and Full Moon

*From the Tower Archive, Ritual Folio, compiled by Brother Moon-Watch, Tidewatch, 1,030 YFH*

Each moon has its own cycle, and each cycle has its own rituals. The rituals described here are the Chorus standard. Individual Towers and realms may have local variations.

[Ed. Note] I have focused on the major rituals: full moon and new moon. The waxing and waning phases have their own minor rituals, but they are too numerous to catalog completely.

## FULL MOON RITUALS

### Emberhowl Full Moon: The Surge

**What happens:** Flame magic becomes uncontrollable. A simple warming spell might incinerate a room. A controlled burn might become a wildfire.

**Ritual:** The Flame Order locks down the Forge Spire. All non-essential fires are extinguished. Apprentices are confined to their quarters. Only Forgesworn and above are allowed to channel magic, and only in the central forge, which is designed to contain surges.

**Civilian practice:** Civilians avoid fire on Emberhowl full moon. No candles. No cooking fires. No lanterns. The Ember Wastes declare a "dark night" — everything is lit by bioluminescent moss, which is not affected by the surge.

**Historical note:** In 750 YFH, an Emberhowl full moon surge burned down half of the Violet Highlands. The fire was so intense that it melted stone. The melted stone was later carved into the Archivum's later foundation reinforcement.

### Solaris Full Moon: The Blaze

**What happens:** Truth becomes unavoidable. Lies are physically uncomfortable to tell. Hidden things become visible.

**Ritual:** The Radiant Order performs mass purification. Corrupted objects, tainted food, and dishonest documents are brought to the Solar Spire and exposed to Solaris's full light. The light reveals what is true and what is false.

**Civilian practice:** This is the traditional night for confession. Dogs who have lied, stolen, or betrayed their packs often confess on Solaris full moon, because the lie becomes too heavy to carry.

**Historical note:** In 920 YFH, a Solaris full moon exposed a Chorus Council member who had been embezzling Order funds. He confessed spontaneously during a council meeting. The full moon was shining through the window.

### Deepwell Full Moon: The Flood

**What happens:** Visions overwhelm. Prophecies come unbidden. Dogs who are not prophets may see flashes of the future. The scrying pools overflow with images.

**Ritual:** The Deep Order opens the Tidewatch to the public. Any dog can receive a prophecy. The Deep Wizards act as interpreters, helping civilians understand what they have seen.

**Civilian practice:** Many dogs avoid sleeping on Deepwell full moon, fearing prophetic nightmares. Those who seek visions drink deepwell water before bed.

**Historical note:** In 1,000 YFH, the Millennium Concordance coincided with a Deepwell full moon. The combined effect produced visions so intense that three dogs went permanently blind. They claimed to have "seen too much."

### Evergreen Full Moon: The Bloom

**What happens:** Growth accelerates wildly. Plants may grow several feet in a single night. Untended gardens become jungles.

**Ritual:** The Wild Order performs "guidance growths" — deliberate magical plantings that use the surge to create useful structures: living bridges, edible walls, medicinal groves.

**Civilian practice:** Farmers plant on Evergreen full moon. The surge guarantees a strong crop. But they must be careful: without Wild Order guidance, the crop might grow too fast and choke itself.

**Historical note:** In 850 YFH, an Evergreen full moon created a "growth storm" in the Deepwood. A single acorn grew into a full-sized oak in six hours. The oak still stands. It is the largest tree in Pawtheon.

### Umbra Full Moon: The Reveal

**What happens:** Secrets surface. Hidden doors become visible. Invisible ink becomes legible. Disguises fail.

**Ritual:** The Arcane Order performs "archival revelation" — exposing old documents to Umbra's full light to reveal hidden text. The Violet Citadel's Restricted Archive is opened for one night only.

**Civilian practice:** This is the night of "unmasking parties," where dogs wear masks and attempt to discover each other's true identities. It is considered good fun, except by dogs with actual secrets.

**Historical note:** In 1,050 YFH, an Umbra full moon revealed a hidden chamber beneath the Forge Spire. The chamber contained pre-Howl artifacts that challenged the official creation narrative. The chamber was resealed and its existence classified.

### Roseglow Full Moon: The Dreamstorm

**What happens:** Collective dreaming. Hundreds of dogs may share the same dream. Dream-threads become visible as physical strands of light.

**Ritual:** The Dream Order hosts a controlled group dream. Participation is voluntary but highly popular. The Dream Wizards guide the dream to prevent nightmares from spreading.

**Civilian practice:** Dogs who do not want to participate sleep with dream-wards: small crystals tuned to block Roseglow's frequency. The crystals are sold by the Dream Order.

**Historical note:** In 1,070 YFH, a Roseglow full moon produced a dreamstorm so intense that it affected dogs who were not asleep. Awake dogs reported "daydreaming" — seeing dream-images while fully conscious. The effect lasted three days.

### Palehowl Full Moon: The Alignment

**What happens:** All magic is enhanced. Even civilian dogs may feel a surge of energy. The Gray Dog is most active during Palehowl full moon.

**Ritual:** There is no formal ritual. Palehowl full moon is a night of possibility. Dogs are encouraged to try new things, start new projects, and take risks.

**Civilian practice:** The Starter Lands hosts a festival on every Palehowl full moon. It is the most popular recurring event in Pawtheon.

**Historical note:** The Lunar Convergence — when all seven moons are full — is essentially a supercharged Palehowl full moon. The last Convergence was in 1,196 YFH. The next will be in 1,691 YFH.

## NEW MOON RITUALS

### Emberhowl New Moon: The Drought

**What happens:** No fire magic. Flame Wizards cannot create fire. They cannot warm themselves. They cannot light a candle.

**Ritual:** The Flame Order practices "dry-fire" — non-magical fire-starting using flint, friction, and chemical reactions. The Forge Spire's furnaces are stoked with conventional fuel.

**Civilian practice:** Civilians are encouraged to practice fire safety. The Ember Wastes hold "dark-fire" competitions: contests to see who can start a fire without magic.

### Solaris New Moon: The Dim

**What happens:** Prophecy fails. Scrying pools are murky. Truth-revelation spells do not work.

**Ritual:** The Radiant Order practices "inner light" — meditation and introspection without magical aid. The Solar Spire's light-towers are turned off.

**Civilian practice:** This is the traditional night for silent reflection. Dogs are encouraged to sit in darkness and simply... think.

### Deepwell New Moon: The Dry

**What happens:** Scrying pools are dark. Prophecy is impossible. The Tidewatch is effectively blind.

**Ritual:** The Deep Order performs "dry-divination" — reading omens from natural signs: cloud patterns, animal behavior, the flow of rivers.

**Civilian practice:** The Abyssal Reaches hold "river-listening" sessions: dogs sit by the water and simply listen, believing that the river speaks even when magic is silent.

### Evergreen New Moon: The Dormant

**What happens:** Ordinary plants continue growing. Magically accelerated growth fails. The Heartwood tree stops humming.

**Ritual:** The Wild Order performs "seed-keeping" — preserving seeds and planning for the next growth cycle. No planting. No growth. Only preparation.

**Civilian practice:** Farmers rest. They do not plant. They do not harvest. They maintain their tools and plan for the next season.

### Umbra New Moon: The Veil

**What happens:** Hidden things stay hidden. Secret doors remain secret. Invisible ink remains invisible.

**Ritual:** The Arcane Order performs "veil-weaving" — creating new secrets, encoding new documents, hiding new chambers. The Violet Citadel's architects do their best work on Umbra new moon.

**Civilian practice:** This is the night for private conversations. Dogs who need to discuss secrets without fear of revelation choose Umbra new moon.

### Roseglow New Moon: The Wake

**What happens:** No magically induced or shared dreams. Ordinary sleep and dreaming continue; the silence of dream-threads can feel restful or disorienting.

**Ritual:** The Dream Order performs "wake-walking" — staying awake for the entire night and engaging in creative work. Artists, writers, and musicians produce their best work on Roseglow new moon.

**Civilian practice:** Many dogs choose to sleep through Roseglow new moon, enjoying rest without magically shared dreams. Others stay awake, fearing the emptiness.

### Palehowl New Moon: The Absence

**What happens:** All magic is weakened. Even the most powerful Wizards feel sluggish. The Current is faint.

**Ritual:** There is no formal ritual. Palehowl new moon is a night of humility. Dogs are reminded that magic is borrowed, not owned.

**Civilian practice:** The Mute holds its largest gatherings on Palehowl new moon. They believe the weakened magic proves their point: dogs do not need the moons to survive.
