# HERETICAL SECTS
## Schisms, Splinters, and Forbidden Beliefs Within the Orders

## EDITOR'S NOTE
*From the Tower Archive, Heresiological Folio, compiled by Brother Arcane-Watch, 1,120 YFH*

Heresy in Pawtheon is not what the Mute believes. The Mute rejects magic entirely. That is apostasy, not heresy. Heresy is when a dog accepts the moons but interprets them wrong.

[Ed. Note] I have recorded seven active sects and three extinct ones. The Chorus Council considers all of them dangerous. The Scribepack considers all of them fascinating. I consider all of them inevitable. Any belief system as old as the Orders will produce variations. The question is not whether heresy exists. The question is whether the Orders can tolerate it.

## I. THE ASHEN FLAME (Flame Order Schism)

### Belief

The Ashen Flame believe that fire should not be controlled. Fire that is controlled is not fire. It is a tool. And tools can be owned. And ownership corrupts.

The Ashen Flame reject the Forge Spire's central furnaces. They reject the Trial by Fire. They reject the very concept of "measured burn." They believe that Flame Wizards should let their fire burn as it wills, even if that means burning themselves.

### Founder

Blazefree, a Flame Wizard who underwent the Trial by Fire in 900 YFH. She passed. She should have become a Flamekeeper. Instead, she walked into the central forge and opened every valve. The Spire nearly burned down. She stood in the center of the inferno, laughing, until the Highforge knocked her unconscious and dragged her out.

When she woke, she said: "Now THAT was fire."

### Practices

- **The Uncontrolled Burn:** Ashen Flame Wizards channel magic without discipline. Their fires are wild, beautiful, and extremely dangerous.
- **The Scarring:** Ashen Flame Wizards wear their burns as badges of honor. A Wizard with unburned fur is considered cowardly.
- **The Purge:** Once a year, the Ashen Flame gather in the Ember Wastes and burn something large: a forest, a building, a mountain of collected objects. The burn is not destructive. It is devotional.

### Status

The Ashen Flame are officially banned by the Flame Order. Approximately 30 adherents practice in secret. The Flame Order estimates that the Ashen Flame are responsible for 12% of accidental magical fires.

## II. THE DIM SEERS (Radiant Order Schism)

### Belief

The Dim Seers believe that light is not the opposite of darkness. Light is a form of darkness that has been excited into visibility. True Radiance is not illumination. It is the ability to see in perfect dark.

The Dim Seers reject the Solar Spire's light-towers. They practice magic in total darkness, believing that only in the absence of light can a dog perceive the "true frequencies" of the world.

### Founder

Darkglow, a Radiant Wizard who went blind in 1,010 YFH. She continued to practice light magic for three years after losing her sight, claiming that she could "see" light more clearly without her eyes interfering. The Highseer ordered her to stop, fearing she would injure herself or others.

She refused. She founded the Dim Seers in the Shadowmire, where darkness is permanent.

### Practices

- **The Blind Purification:** Dim Seers perform healing and cleansing rituals in total darkness. They claim their magic is more effective because it is not distracted by visual appearances.
- **The Eye-Vow:** New Dim Seers voluntarily blindfold themselves for one year. If they survive, they are accepted as full members.
- **The Shadow-Light:** Dim Seers have developed a form of magic that illuminates only what is hidden. A Shadow-Light spell reveals secret doors, concealed weapons, and invisible ink. It does not illuminate ordinary objects.

### Status

The Dim Seers are tolerated, barely, by the Radiant Order. They are useful: their Shadow-Light magic is unmatched for espionage and security. But they are considered embarrassing. "A Radiant Wizard who hides in darkness," the Highseer said, "is like a fish who climbs trees."

Approximately 15 adherents practice openly. Another 20 practice in secret within the Solar Spire itself.

## III. THE BLOOD TIDES (Deep Order Schism)

### Belief

The Blood Tides believe that water is not the proper medium for prophecy. Blood is. Water is external. Blood is internal. Water reflects the world. Blood reflects the self. True prophecy requires looking inward, not outward.

The Blood Tides reject the Tidewatch's scrying pools. They practice blood-divination: cutting a small wound and reading the patterns of the flow. They claim this produces more accurate prophecies because blood cannot lie to itself.

### Founder

Crimsonpool, a Deep Wizard who became frustrated with the Tidewatch's accuracy requirements. She predicted twelve events correctly using water-scrying, but each time, another prophet had predicted the same event first. She was never promoted to Tided.

In 1,080 YFH, she cut her own paw during a frustrated meditation session and saw, in the blood, the exact moment of her own death. The vision was so precise that she recorded it in the Tidewatch archives. It has not yet come true.

### Practices

- **The Blood Scry:** A small cut is made on the paw. The blood is allowed to drip onto a flat stone. The patterns are interpreted as visions.
- **The Shared Cut:** Blood Tides in a group will cut each other simultaneously, believing that mingled blood produces shared visions.
- **The Death Prophecy:** Every Blood Tide must prophecy their own death within their first year. They spend the rest of their lives waiting for it to come true.

### Status

The Blood Tides are officially banned. The Chorus Council considers blood-divination "unsanitary and psychologically destabilizing." The Tidewatch considers it "competition."

Approximately 10 adherents practice in secret. The Tidewatch has been trying to locate them for 40 years without success.

## IV. THE ROT GARDENERS (Wild Order Schism)

### Belief

The Rot Gardeners believe that the Great Rot is not a disease. It is an evolution. The Shadowmire's rot is simply a faster, more aggressive form of growth. What the Wild Order calls "corruption," the Rot Gardeners call "acceleration."

They believe that Wild Wizards should not fight the Rot. They should cultivate it. A forest that grows in days instead of decades is not a problem. It is a solution.

### Founder

Decaybloom, a Wild Wizard who was assigned to the Shadowmire in 1,030 YFH to "contain" the Great Rot. She spent ten years trying to purge it. She failed. The Rot kept growing back, faster each time.

In 1,040 YFH, she stopped fighting. She began studying the Rot. She learned that it was not random decay. It was patterned. Organized. Almost... intelligent. She began communicating with it. Not with words. With growth. She would grow a plant, and the Rot would reshape it. She would grow another, and the Rot would reshape it differently.

She concluded that the Rot was not destroying life. It was offering a different kind of life.

### Practices

- **Rot-Cultivation:** Rot Gardeners grow plants in Rot-infected soil. The plants that survive are faster-growing, more resilient, and sometimes sentient.
- **The Rot-Bond:** A Rot Gardener can form a bond with a specific Rot colony, similar to a Dream Wizard's dream-thread. The bond allows limited communication and control.
- **The Transformation:** Some Rot Gardeners deliberately infect themselves with small amounts of Rot, believing that controlled infection produces enhanced growth magic.

### Status

The Rot Gardeners are considered extremely dangerous. The Wild Order has burned three Rot-Gardener colonies. The Heartwood tree itself — the sacred center of the Wild Order — has produced a root that kills Rot on contact.

Approximately 20 adherents practice in the Shadowmire. They are not welcome in any other realm.

## V. THE LOOPED (Arcane Order Schism)

### Belief

The Looped believe that time is not linear. It is circular. Every event has already happened and will happen again. Free will is an illusion. The Arcane Order's research into temporal magic is pointless because the future is fixed.

The Looped do not try to change the future. They try to remember it.

### Founder

Again-And, an Arcane Wizard who spent twenty years studying temporal magic. She developed a technique that allowed her to experience brief "time-echoes" — moments from the past or future that overlapped with the present.

The experience broke her. She became convinced that she was living the same life over and over, and that her research was merely a repetition of research she had already done in previous cycles.

She founded the Looped in 1,050 YFH. Her followers believe she has remembered more cycles than any other dog.

### Practices

- **The Recollection:** Looped Wizards meditate on specific moments, trying to "remember" what happened before they happened. Success is measured by the accuracy of predictions that are not prophecies but memories.
- **The Acceptance:** Looped Wizards do not resist fate. If a Looped Wizard remembers that they will die on a specific day, they arrange their affairs and wait calmly.
- **The Loop-Break:** A minority of the Looped believe that there IS a way to break the cycle, but it requires an action so unpredictable that it has never been tried in any previous loop. They have been debating what that action might be for 150 years.

### Status

The Looped are tolerated by the Arcane Order because they are harmless. They do not disrupt research. They simply believe it is futile. The Highthink considers them "philosophically interesting but practically useless."

Approximately 25 adherents practice openly.

## VI. THE UNWOVEN (Dream Order Schism)

### Belief

The Unwoven believe that dream-threads are not connections. They are prisons. When two dogs form a dream-thread, they bind their fates together. They limit each other's possibilities. True freedom requires cutting all threads.

The Unwoven reject the Dream Spire's emphasis on bonding. They practice "solitary dreaming" — entering the dream realm alone, without threads, without anchors. It is dangerous. The dream realm without threads is vast, chaotic, and hungry. But the Unwoven believe it is the only honest way to dream.

### Founder

Alone-Always, a Dream Wizard who was deeply bonded to three packmates. In 1,070 YFH, all three died in a Mud Rot outbreak. The severing of the dream-threads nearly killed her. She survived, but she refused to form new threads.

She claimed that the trauma had shown her the truth: threads are beautiful until they break. And they always break.

### Practices

- **The Thread-Cutting:** New Unwoven must cut all existing dream-threads. This is painful and permanent. Some dogs never recover emotionally.
- **The Solitary Walk:** Unwoven Wizards enter the dream realm alone. They report visions that no threaded Dream Wizard has ever seen: cities of ice, oceans of stars, dogs that are not dogs.
- **The Refusal:** Unwoven refuse to participate in pack-casting. They will not amplify other Wizards' magic. They consider amplification "theft of individual potential."

### Status

The Unwoven are officially banned. The Dream Order considers them "self-destructive and anti-social." The Highdream has called them "dogs who have chosen to be alone in the most dangerous place in existence."

Approximately 8 adherents practice openly. The Dream Order believes another 15 practice in secret.

## VII. THE MOON-CHOSEN (Cross-Order Schism)

### Belief

The Moon-Chosen believe that the Sorting Ceremony is wrong. Dogs should not be assigned to one moon. They should serve ALL moons. A dog who channels only fire is half a dog. A dog who sees only one color is blind.

The Moon-Chosen reject the Order system entirely. They practice magic from multiple moons, believing that cross-channeling produces effects that no single Order can achieve.

### Founder

All-Six, a Cross-Tone dog who was sorted into the Flame Order but never stopped feeling the other five moons. She trained secretly with Wizards from every Order, learning the basics of each discipline.

In 1,090 YFH, she publicly demonstrated a spell that combined fire, light, water, growth, dimensional shift, and dream-weaving. The effect was spectacular and uncontrollable. She destroyed a quarter of the Starter Lands and vanished. She has not been seen since.

### Practices

- **Cross-Channeling:** Moon-Chosen attempt to channel multiple moons simultaneously. The effects are unpredictable and usually dangerous.
- **The Moon-Circle:** Moon-Chosen gather at the lunar convergence and attempt to channel all seven moons at once. No one has succeeded.
- **The Hat Collection:** Some Moon-Chosen try to acquire multiple hats. This does not work. A hat bonded to one dog will not function for another. But the Moon-Chosen keep trying.

### Status

The Moon-Chosen are banned by all six Orders. The Chorus Council considers them "existentially threatening" because their cross-channeling could destabilize the Current.

Approximately 5 adherents practice openly. The Council believes there may be 20-30 in secret.

## EXTINCT SECTS

### The Frozen Flame (Flame Order, 800-850 YFH)

Believed that fire and ice were the same element in different states. Attempted to create "cold fire." The sect died out when its founder froze himself solid during an experiment.

### The Hollow Seers (Deep Order, 700-720 YFH)

Believed that the Hollow was a place that could be visited through deep meditation. Twenty-three sect pilgrims attempted the journey. None returned. The sect dissolved.

### The Twin Moons (Cross-Order, 500-600 YFH)

Believed that each moon had a twin that was invisible. Spent a century searching for the "hidden moons." Found nothing. The sect faded into obscurity.
