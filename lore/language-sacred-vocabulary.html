SACRED VOCABULARY

Fifty Words of the Urfic Tongue

[Edition note] Revised1200YFH edition. Original compiler dates below identify earlier textual layers; later events and citations belong to this revision. Canon Contract continuity settlement governs literal chronology, seat counts and physical rules.

EDITOR'S NOTE

From the Tower Archive, Linguistic Folio, compiled by Brother Urfic-Keep, Scribepack, 1,050 YFH

Urfic is the liturgical language of Pawtheon. It is not spoken in daily life. It is howled, hummed, and scratched into stone. The following fifty words are the most commonly used in sacred texts, rituals, and Tower inscriptions.

[Ed. Note] Urfic has no written alphabet in the conventional sense. It is recorded in howl-notation: marks indicating pitch, duration, and breath. The transliterations below are approximations. Some tones have no equivalent in spoken language.

--- Brother Urfic-Keep

GRAMMATICAL NOTES

Word Order

Urfic uses a subject-object-verb structure:

  • "Urf-vel korr-marur" = Primal-one stone-silence = "The primal one walked through silent stone."

Possession

Possession is indicated by hyphenation:

  • "Pack-urfa" = Pack-primal = "The pack's primal" or "The primal of the pack."

Tense

Urfic does not distinguish past, present, and future. All statements are timeless. Context determines tense:

  • "Vox-marur urfa-se" = Howl-silence primal-from = "From the silence, the howl" (could be past, present, or future).

Negation

Negation is indicated by prefixing "Na-" to the word:

  • "Na-vox" = Not-howl = Silence.
  • "Na-loyalty" = Not-loyalty = Betrayal.

The Sacred Vowel Rule

Every sacred word must contain at least one of the seven sacred vowels. Words without sacred vowels are considered "hollow" — empty of meaning, dangerous to speak in ritual contexts.

The Mute has adopted this rule. Their doctrine states that the sacred vowels represent the moons' enslavement, and "hollow words" — words without sacred vowels — are the only truly free speech.

SAMPLE TEXTS

The First Howl Hymn (Excerpt)

Urf-marur. Urf-marur.
A-O-U-E-I-AE-Y.
Vox-urfa marur-se.

Transliteration: Primal-silence. Primal-silence. A-O-U-E-I-AE-Y. Howl-primal silence-from.

Translation: From the silence, the primal howl. A, O, U, E, I, AE, Y. From the silence, the primal howl.

The Hat Oath

Hat-se current-vox.
Pack-loyalty hat-korr.
Na-hat urfa-se.

Transliteration: Hat-from current-howl. Pack-loyalty hat-stone. Not-hat primal-from.

Translation: From the hat, the current howls. The pack's loyalty is the hat's stone. From the not-hat, the primal.

(Smoothed: "I accept what was given. The pack's loyalty is my foundation. Without the hat, I am primal.")

The Mute Rejection

Na-vox. Na-current. Na-hat.
Urf-vel na-moon-se.
Pack-loyalty na-magic-se.

Transliteration: Not-howl. Not-current. Not-hat. Primal-one not-moon-from. Pack-loyalty not-magic-from.

Translation: No howl. No current. No hat. The primal one is not from the moon. The pack's loyalty is not from magic.

(Smoothed: "We reject the howl, the current, and the hat. True devotion does not come from the moons. True loyalty does not require magic.")
