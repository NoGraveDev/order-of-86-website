## EDITOR'S NOTE
*From the Tower Archive, Calendrical Folio, compiled by Sister Calendar-Keep, 1,010 YFH*

The Pawtheon calendar is based on the moons. It was formalized in 600 YFH when the Chorus Council realized that each Order was celebrating on different days, causing confusion and missed meetings.

[Ed. Note] The calendar described here is the Chorus standard. Individual realms and Orders may have local variations. The Frosthollow Huskies, for example, celebrate the winter solstice three days earlier than the official date because their astronomical observations are more precise.

## THE CALENDAR SYSTEM

### Year Structure

- **Days per year:** 364
- **Weeks per year:** 52
- **Days per week:** 7
- **Months per year:** 13
- **Days per month:** 28

The 364-day year contains precisely52weeks and13civil months, not13physical lunar cycles. There is no leap year. The calendar is stable.

### Days of the Week

| Day | Name | Moon | Significance |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| 1 | A-day | Emberhowl | Day of action, courage, new beginnings |
| 2 | O-day | Solaris | Day of truth, clarity, judgment |
| 3 | U-day | Deepwell | Day of depth, introspection, prophecy |
| 4 | E-day | Evergreen | Day of growth, labor, community |
| 5 | I-day | Umbra | Day of secrets, study, hidden work |
| 6 | AE-day | Roseglow | Day of dreams, rest, creativity |
| 7 | Y-day | Palehowl | Day of possibility, rest, freedom |

Y-day is the universal rest day. No Order holds formal meetings on Y-day. Pilgrim Packs do not walk on Y-day. The Scribepack does not copy on Y-day.

The Gray Dog, characteristically, walks on Y-day.

### Months of the Year

| Month | Name | Moon | Significance |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| 1 | Ember-rise | Emberhowl | New year. Courage and fire. |
| 2 | Solar-bright | Solaris | Light and truth. |
| 3 | Deep-flow | Deepwell | Water and prophecy. |
| 4 | Green-sprout | Evergreen | Growth and planting. |
| 5 | Umbr-shadow | Umbra | Secrets and study. |
| 6 | Rose-bloom | Roseglow | Dreams and love. |
| 7 | Pale-wane | Palehowl | Possibility and reflection. |
| 8 | Ember-fall | Emberhowl | Harvest and preservation. |
| 9 | Solar-dim | Solaris | Preparation and honesty. |
| 10 | Deep-freeze | Deepwell | Winter and foresight. |
| 11 | Green-sleep | Evergreen | Rest and memory. |
| 12 | Umbr-long | Umbra | Darkness and knowledge. |
| 13 | Rose-pale | Roseglow & Palehowl | The double month. Dreams and possibility. |

The13thmonth, Rose-pale, symbolically honors Roseglow and Palehowl together. Their physical joint full phase recurs every330days and drifts across civil months; the festival does not promise an alignment.

## MAJOR HOLY DAYS

### The Concordance (Summer Solstice, E-day of Ember-rise)

**Significance:** The most important religious day in Pawtheon. Commemorates the First Howl.

**Official Story:** All six Order Towers ring simultaneously, producing a harmonic resonance that can be felt across all seven realms. The Gray Dog howls along. The effect strengthens the Loyalty Network and purifies the Current.

**Actual Practice:** The Concordance is spectacular but exhausting. Wizards channel magic continuously for twelve hours. Civilians gather in the Starter Lands for a festival. There is food, music, and — inevitably — political speeches.

**The Gray Dog's Role:** The Gray Dog howls at dawn and dusk. He does not participate in the political speeches.

**The Mute's Response:** The Mute holds a Counter-Silence on the Concordance. They gather in the Shadowmire and sit in absolute silence for the entire day. They call it "The True Concordance."

### The Attunement (Spring Equinox, O-day of Green-sprout)

**Significance:** A day of personal reflection and "retuning."

**Practice:** Dogs visit their Order's Tower and participate in a brief ritual: striking a personal object (a stone, a piece of wood, a crystal) and listening to its decay tone. The tone is used as a focus for moral reflection.

The Mute practices a version without magic: they strike stones and listen to the natural resonance, using it as a meditation focus.

### The Silence (Winter Solstice, U-day of Deep-freeze)

**Significance:** Originally a day of quiet meditation. Now also the anniversary of the Great Silence.

**Practice:** The Chorus Council observes three hours of silence at midday. The Orders do not channel magic during this period. Civilians are encouraged to spend the day in quiet reflection.

**The Mute's Response:** The Mute considers this day their most sacred. They hold their annual vigil at the unnamed mountain, waiting for the Hollow to return.

**Controversy:** Some Wizards believe the Silence should be a day of mourning for the Great Silence's dead. Others believe it should be a day of gratitude for the magic's return. The Chorus Council has refused to take a position.

### The Sovereign's Festival (Autumn Equinox, A-day of Ember-fall)

**Significance:** A civic celebration, not religious. Commemorates the founding of the Chorus Council.

**Practice:** Parades in all seven realms. Competitions: magical duels (formalized, non-lethal), pack races, howling contests. Public meals. Fireworks in the Ember Wastes.

**The Gray Dog's Role:** He usually attends the Starter Lands parade. He walks at the back, behind the last float. Children throw flowers at him. He catches them in his mouth and drops them on the ground.

### The Lunar Convergence (Variable, occurs once every495civil years)

**Significance:** When all seven moons are full simultaneously.

**Practice:** The Lunar Convergence is the most magical night in Pawtheon. Palehowl blazes brighter than all six Order Moons combined. The Current surges. Wizards report enhanced power, clearer prophecy, and vivid dreams.

The Gray Dog is most active during the Convergence. He walks between all six Towers in a single night, something he never does at any other time. He speaks more during the Convergence than in the entire rest of the decade.

**The Mute's Response:** The Mute hides during the Convergence. They believe the surge of magic is dangerous and unstable.

### The Hat-Blessing (Variable, occurs on a Wizard's sorting anniversary)

**Significance:** Each Wizard celebrates the anniversary of the day their hat found them.

**Practice:** The Wizard returns to the Starter Lands and stands in the Center Circle. They remove their hat, hold it to the sky, and speak the Hat Oath:

> "I accept what was given. I serve what gave it. I am a channel, not a source. I am a lens, not a light. I remember that this hat is borrowed, and one day it will be returned."

The Wizard then places the hat back on their head and channels a single spell — their simplest, most basic spell — to remind themselves of where they began.

**The Mute's Response:** The Mute considers the Hat-Blessing "theatrical self-abasement." They hold a parallel ceremony called the Brim-Breaking, where Mute dogs strike stones together to symbolize the breaking of their own "hats" — the mental chains they believe the Orders impose.

## ORDER-SPECIFIC FEAST DAYS

### Flame Order: The Forge Day (A-day of Ember-rise)

The Forge Spire opens its doors to civilians. Anyone can enter and watch the Wizards work. Children are given small pieces of metal to shape with their paws. The day ends with the Great Ignition: all furnaces are stoked to maximum simultaneously, producing a pillar of fire visible from three realms away.

### Radiant Order: The Purification Day (O-day of Solar-bright)

The Solar Spire performs mass purification rituals. Sick dogs, corrupted objects, and tainted food are brought to the Spire and cleansed. The line often stretches for miles. The Radiant Wizards work until they collapse from exhaustion.

### Deep Order: The Pool-Opening (U-day of Deep-flow)

The Tidewatch opens its scrying pools to the public. Any dog can ask a question and receive a prophecy. The prophecies are brief and often cryptic: "The path you fear is the path you need." "Do not trust the dog who smiles with only one side of their mouth." The Deep Wizards do not explain. They simply speak.

### Wild Order: The Heartwood Bloom (E-day of Green-sprout)

The Heartwood tree produces a single day of accelerated blooming. Flowers appear on branches that have been bare for decades. The scent is intoxicating. Dogs from all realms travel to the Heartwood Spire to experience it. The Wild Order distributes seeds to pilgrims.

### Arcane Order: The Open Archive (I-day of Umbr-shadow)

The Violet Citadel opens its archives to the public. Anyone can read anything — except the Restricted section. The Arcane Wizards spend the day explaining complex theories to confused civilians. It is their favorite day of the year.

### Dream Order: The Shared Sleep (AE-day of Rose-bloom)

The Dream Spire invites civilians to participate in a controlled group dream. The experience is overwhelming: hundreds of minds sharing a single dream-space. Most participants wake up crying. Almost all of them say it was beautiful.

## REALM-SPECIFIC TRADITIONS

### Frosthollow: The Ice Howl (Y-day of Deep-freeze)

The Huskies gather on the Frozen Lake of Echoes and howl in unison. The ice reflects the sound, creating a chorus that lasts for minutes after the howling stops. The tradition predates the First Howl.

### Ember Wastes: The Ash Garden (A-day of Ember-fall)

The Tigers scatter volcanic ash on a designated field and plant fire-resistant seeds. The ash garden blooms for one week, producing flowers that are beautiful and mildly toxic. The Tigers consider them sacred.

### Sunward Heights: The Shadow Dance (I-day of Solar-bright)

The Solids perform a dance in which they perform silhouette theater with cutouts against lit cloth, rather than casting natural shadows in the shadowless Heights. The dance tells the story of the First Howl without words. It is the oldest continuous performance tradition in Pawtheon.

### Deepwood: The Root-Walk (E-day of Green-sleep)

The Collies walk barefoot through the forest at night, following the root systems of the oldest trees. The belief is that the roots remember every dog who has ever walked them, and walking them connects you to the past.

### Abyssal Reaches: The Twin Boats (U-day of Deep-flow)

The Splits release two boats — one upstream, one downstream — carrying offerings for the dead. Whichever boat sinks first is believed to indicate which half of the spirit is stronger.

### Violet Highlands: The Archivum Open Day (I-day of Umbr-shadow)

The Archivum opens its rarest documents to the public. Dogs line up for days to see the original obsidian tablets of the First Howl Hymn.

### Shadowmire: The Rot-Watch (AE-day of Rose-pale)

The Zombies gather at the edge of the Great Rot and watch it spread for one hour. They do not try to stop it. They simply observe. The belief is that the Rot is trying to communicate, and observation is the first step toward understanding.
