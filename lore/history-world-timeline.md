# WORLD TIMELINE
## The History of Pawtheon from Silence to the Present

---

[Edition note] Revised1200YFH edition. Original compiler dates below identify earlier textual layers; later events and citations belong to this revision. Canon Contract continuity settlement governs literal chronology, seat counts and physical rules.

## EDITOR'S NOTE
*From the Tower Archive, Chronological Folio, compiled by Brother Scribepack-Time, 1,200 YFH*

The following timeline is the best reconstruction available. It draws on archaeological evidence, pack-memory fragments, Order records, and — where necessary — informed guesswork.

[Ed. Note] Dates before 400 YFH are approximate. Dates after 400 YFH are generally reliable. Dates after 1,000 YFH are considered current history.

The timeline is presented in two layers: the **Official Story** (what the Chorus Council teaches) and the **Deeper Record** (what the archives actually contain).

## PRE-HISTORY (Before 0 YFH)

### Official Story
Before the First Howl, there was nothing worth recording. Dogs were ordinary animals. They hunted. They bred. They died. They had no language, no culture, no souls.

### Deeper Record
The pre-Howl dogs had language (howl-patterns and scent-complexes). They had culture (the Old Pack Ways). They had spirituality (the Silent Gods, the Long Run). They simply did not write.

Archaeological evidence suggests that dogs had been evolving toward social complexity for at least 50,000 years before the First Howl. The Gray Dog did not create intelligence. He accelerated it.

## THE HOWLING AGE (0–400 YFH)
### 0 YFH — The First Howl

**Official Story:** The Gray Dog sat on the unnamed mountain and howled. The seven moons woke. Magic poured into the world. Dogs began to evolve.

**Deeper Record:** The First Howl was not instantaneous. The earliest standing is dated to10–20YFH and uncontrolled channeling to20–30YFH. Upright culture became widespread by200YFH; deliberate but hazardous focus experiments spread by300YFH. Controlled personal spell shaping came with the hats. The "event" of the First Howl was actually a process.

The Gray Dog did not howl once. He howled continuously for three days. Then he stopped. Then he howled again. Then he stopped. For the first century, he howled once per year, on the anniversary of the first howl. These annual howls are called the Echo Howls.
### 50 YFH — The First Listeners

Dogs with unusually sensitive hearing begin reporting that they can "feel" the moons. They are not yet Wizards. They are simply dogs who pay attention. These are the spiritual ancestors of the Deep Order.
### 120 YFH — The First Well-Attested Fire-Starter

A dog in the Ember Wastes accidentally channels Emberhowl's frequency while trying to warm her pups. She creates fire without flint or friction. She does not understand what she did. She is terrified. She tells no one.

The story spreads anyway. Other dogs try to replicate the effect. Most fail. A few succeed. The first "wild mages" appear.
### 200 YFH — The First Pack of Mages

Twelve dogs who can channel magic gather in the Deepwood. They form a pack based not on blood relations but on shared ability. This is the first magical community. It lasts three years before internal conflict destroys it.

The lesson: magic without structure is dangerous.
### 250 YFH — The Echo Howls Cease

The Gray Dog stops his annual howling. He does not explain why. He simply sits on the unnamed mountain and watches.

Some dogs believe the moons are now stable enough to sustain themselves. Others believe the Gray Dog is tired. Others believe he is waiting for something.
### 300 YFH — The First Experimental Focus Towers

Dogs begin building structures to focus the Current. The first Tower is built in the Ember Wastes: a stone spire tuned to Emberhowl's frequency. It is crude. It is dangerous. It channels fire so powerfully that the builders are incinerated.

But it works. Other experimental focus towers follow. By350YFH there are prototypes in every realm. These are not the six named Order Spires foreseen in ChapterII; those are founded after the hats arrive.
### 350 YFH — The First Conflict

Two magical communities in the Violet Highlands go to war over control of a ley-line intersection. The war lasts six months. Forty dogs die. The ley-line is destroyed.

The lesson: magical communities need a neutral arbiter.

## THE HAT AGE (400–600 YFH)
### 400 YFH — The Second Howl

**Official Story:** The Gray Dog howled a second time. The six Order moons dropped hats. Eighty-five dogs were chosen. The Gray Dog received the last hat. Palehowl created the Starter Lands.

**Deeper Record:** The Second Howl was not planned. The Gray Dog had been watching the Towers for fifty years. He saw the wild mages. He saw the conflicts. He saw the potential and the danger.

He howled not to create Wizards but to focus magic. The hats were not gifts. They were filters. The Gray Dog designed them — or channeled them from the moons — to limit each Wizard to one moon's frequency. This prevented the chaos of wild magic.

The Starter Lands were created as a training ground because the Gray Dog knew that eighty-five Wizards with focused power would still need discipline.
### 410 YFH — The Sorting Ceremony Established

The first formal Sorting Ceremony is held in the Starter Lands. Twelve young dogs are tested. Seven are chosen by moons to succeed to seven vacated bonds; no new seats are created. Five are not. The five become the first Tower-Keeps.
### 425 YFH — The First Order Council

The six Order leaders meet for the first time. They do not agree on much. But they agree that conflict between Orders must be prevented. They establish the precursor to the Chorus Council: an informal gathering with no authority but significant influence.
### 450 YFH — The Scribepack Founded

A group of Tower-Keeps in the Violet Highlands formalizes the writing experiments begun around400YFH. They begin recording the oral traditions of the First Howl. The Scribepack is born.
### 475 YFH — The First Pilgrim Pack

A pack of unmagical dogs walks from the Forge Spire to the Solar Spire. They walk not as messengers or traders but as devotees. The Pilgrim Packs are born.
### 500 YFH — The Twin Moons Heresy

A group of Arcane Wizards claims to have discovered evidence of invisible twin moons. They search for a century. They find nothing. The sect dissolves by600YFH.
### 520 YFH — The First Concordance

All six Orders agree to ring their Towers simultaneously on the summer solstice. The effect is spectacular: a harmonic resonance that can be felt across all seven realms. The Concordance becomes an annual tradition.
### 550 YFH — The Heartwood Tree Awakens

The tree at the center of the Heartwood Spire — already ancient — begins producing a low-frequency hum that matches Evergreen's tone. The Wild Order claims the tree has become conscious. The Arcane Order claims it is a natural resonance phenomenon. Both are partly right.
### 580 YFH — The Listener Order Founded

Dogs who believe the Orders have become too focused on action establish the first listening posts. They sit in silence and simply... listen. The Listener Order is born.
### 600 YFH — The Chorus Council Formalized

The informal Order gathering becomes formal. The Chorus Council is established with six Voices (one per Order) and the Concordance Spire is built in the Starter Lands. The Gray Dog is invited to be the Seventh Voice. He accepts the invitation but has never cast a vote.

## THE EXPANSION AGE (600–900 YFH)
### 620 YFH — The First Inter-Realm Road

A resonant road is built between the Violet Highlands and the Abyssal Reaches. It is the first infrastructure project involving multiple Orders. The road hums at a frequency that calms travelers.
### 650 YFH — The Archivum Established

The Violet Highlands formally establishes the Archivum library around the older sacred walls described in ChapterII: a massive institution collecting every known text. The Scribepack moves its headquarters there.
### 680 YFH — The First Cross-Order Marriage

A Flame Wizard and a Deep Wizard form a dream-thread so powerful that they refuse to sever it. They marry. Their pups are born feeling both fire and water frequencies. The Chorus Council debates whether the pups should be sorted into one Order or allowed to choose.

The Gray Dog settles the debate. He visits the family, looks at the pups, and says: "Let them choose when they are ready." The pups are never sorted. They become the first Moon-Chosen.
### 700 YFH — The Hollow Seers

A Deep Order sect claims to have visited the Hollow through meditation. Twenty-three sect pilgrims, not twenty-three bonded Deep Wizards, attempt the journey. None return. The sect is banned.
### 720 YFH — The Hollow Seers Disband

The failed expedition leaves the sect broken. Its remaining adherents disband.
### 750 YFH — The Chorus Guard Founded

An assassination attempt on the First Voice leads to the creation of the Chorus Guard. They are controversial from the beginning.
### 780 YFH — The First Census

The Scribepack attempts to count every dog in Pawtheon. The number is approximately 2.3 million. The census records all85Order seats occupied, alongside the Wanderer. The seat cap, not each bearer’s identity or uninterrupted occupancy, has remained unchanged since the Second Howl.
### 820 YFH — The First Outlands Treaty

The Orders formally recognize the non-magical Outlands tribes as sovereign. The treaty is mostly symbolic — the Orders do not control the Outlands — but it establishes a framework for diplomacy.
### 850 YFH — The Frozen Flame Extinction

The Frozen Flame sect dies out when their founder freezes himself solid. The Flame Order uses the incident as a cautionary tale.
### 850 YFH — The Great Rot Begins

A corruption appears in the Shadowmire. It spreads slowly at first. The Wild Order attempts to contain it. They fail. The Rot becomes a permanent feature of the realm.
### 880 YFH — The First Zombie

A Shadowmire dog dies from Rot exposure but does not stay dead. He reanimates three days later, conscious but physically corrupted. The Shadowmire Zombies are born.
### 900 YFH — The Ashen Flame Rebellion

Blazefree rejects the Trial by Fire and opens every valve in the Forge Spire. The Spire nearly burns down. The Ashen Flame form and are banned; their adherents go underground.

## THE TENSION AGE (900–1,100 YFH)
### 920 YFH — The First Resource Dispute

The Flame Order and the Wild Order argue over control of a volcanic valley that produces both fire-crystals and growth-soil. The dispute lasts five years. The Chorus Council eventually divides the valley in half.
### 940 YFH — The Natural Theory Codified

Brother Wild-Patience publishes "The Dog Who Sat," arguing that the Gray Dog was an ordinary dog who became extraordinary through patience. The theory becomes the Chorus orthodoxy.
### 950 YFH — The First Mute Dog

A dog attends the Sorting Ceremony and feels no moon's frequency. He is sent home. He is not angry. He is simply... quiet. The term "Mute" enters the language. It will be another 150 years before the anti-magic Mute faction adopts the name.
### 990 YFH — The First Prophecy War

Two Deep Wizards prophecy conflicting futures for the same event. Both are correct, but in different ways. The incident sparks a debate about the nature of prophecy that continues to this day.
### 1,000 YFH — The Millennium Concordance

The thousandth anniversary of the First Howl. The Concordance is the largest in history. All six Towers ring for three days. The Gray Dog howls along. For the first time in 750 years, the Echo Howl is heard again.

Dogs across Pawtheon report feeling "more connected" for weeks afterward. The Loyalty Network experiences a measurable surge in strength.
### 1,013 YFH — The Dim Seers Founded

Three years after going blind in1010YFH, Darkglow founds the Dim Seers in the Shadowmire. The Radiant Order tolerates them barely.
### 1,020 YFH — The Radiant Schism

A faction within the Radiant Order claims that the Gray Dog's glasses are proof of extraterrestrial origin. The Highseer suppresses the claim. The faction goes underground.
### 1,040 YFH — The Rot Gardeners Founded

Decaybloom stops fighting the Great Rot and begins studying it. The Rot Gardeners are born.
### 1,050 YFH — The Looped Founded

Again-And breaks from temporal research and founds the Looped. The Arcane Order tolerates them.
### 1,070 YFH — The Unwoven Founded

Alone-Always cuts her dream-threads and founds the Unwoven. The Dream Order bans them.
### 1,080 YFH — The Blood Tides Founded

Crimsonpool discovers blood-divination. The Deep Order bans her. She goes underground.
### 1,090 YFH — The Cross-Channeling Incident

All-Six demonstrates cross-channeling and destroys a quarter of the Starter Lands. She vanishes. The Moon-Chosen are banned by all six Orders.
### 1,090 YFH — The Theological Debates Peak

Sister Arcane-Theory compiles the four competing interpretations of the Gray Dog's origin. The document becomes the most widely read philosophical text in Pawtheon.

## THE SILENCE AGE (1,100–1,200 YFH)
### 1,100 YFH — The Great Silence

For forty-nine hours, all magic stops. The moons go dark. The Current falls silent. The Loyalty Network collapses.

Thousands die. Hospitals fail. Crops wither. Frontier settlements freeze.

The moons return. Magic returns. But nothing is the same.
### 1,102 YFH — The Mute Founded

Ashpaw, having renounced his Flame hat during the1100Silence, formally founds the anti-magic Mute faction. His pamphlet, "The Theft of Silence," spreads through the Shadowmire and the Outlands.
### 1,105 YFH — The Chorus Rebuttal

The Chorus Council publishes "The Gift of Song," refuting the Mute doctrine. The debate begins.
### 1,110 YFH — The Unfinished Dead Study

Sister Deep-Current publishes her study of dogs who died during the Great Silence. She discovers that their magic did not return to the moons. It simply stopped. These are the "unfinished dead."
### 1,120 YFH — The Dim Seers Tolerated

The Radiant Order formally tolerates the Dim Seers, acknowledging their usefulness for espionage. The Highseer calls them "fish who climb trees" but keeps them on retainer.
### 1,130 YFH — Ashpaw Dies

Ashpaw dies in a Mud Rot outbreak. He never wears a hat again. The Mute continues without him.
### 1,140 YFH — The Mute Vigil Established

The Mute begins its annual vigil at the unnamed mountain: sitting in silence on the anniversary of the Great Silence, waiting for the Hollow to return.
### 1,145–1,150 YFH — The Loyalty Wars

Tensions between Orders — exacerbated by the Great Silence and the Mute's recruitment — erupt into open conflict. The Loyalty Wars run from1145 to1150YFH. Two hundred sixty-four Order-affiliated personnel and10,300civilians die; the personnel include bonded Wizards and unbonded staff, not264simultaneous hat-bearers.

The Wars end when the Gray Dog walks onto a battlefield and sits down. Both sides stop fighting. No one knows why.
### 1,155 YFH — The Loyalty Accords

The Orders sign the Loyalty Accords, formally pledging non-aggression. The Accords are simple: "We are one pack. We hunt together. We do not hunt each other."
### 1,160 YFH — The Gray Dog Speaks to the Mute

The Gray Dog visits a Mute camp and makes a rare public statement. His words — "It was a choice. Choices can be questioned" — are debated endlessly.
### 1,180 YFH — The Mute Doctrine Preserved

Brother Tidewatch-Deep copies the Mute Doctrine into the Restricted Archive. His editorial note: "The Mute are not stupid. They are dogs who lost something and never got it back."
### 1,200 YFH — The Present

Pawtheon is at peace. Uneasily.

The Orders function. The Chorus Council meets. The Concordance is celebrated. The Pilgrim Packs walk. The Scribepack copies. The Listeners listen.

But beneath the surface, tensions remain. The Mute grows. The Hollow Theory spreads. The Great Rot advances. And the Gray Dog sits on the unnamed mountain, looking at the seven moons, waiting.

No one knows what he is waiting for. But every dog in Pawtheon knows, in the quiet part of their heart, that he is waiting for something.
