THE FIRST HOWL HYMN

The Oldest Sacred Text of the Pawtheon

[Edition note] Revised1200YFH edition. Original compiler dates below identify earlier textual layers; later events and citations belong to this revision. Canon Contract continuity settlement governs literal chronology, seat counts and physical rules.

EDITOR'S NOTE

From the Tower Archive, Folio Primus, Transcribed by Sister Heartwood-Keep, 847 YFH

The following text is the oldest known transcription of the First Howl Hymn. It was preserved orally for four hundred years before any dog learned to scratch symbols into stone. The version below was recorded by the first Scribepack in the Violet Highlands, who attempted to write what had only ever been sung.

[Howled] The oral tradition varies by pack. This is the most widely accepted version, though the Ember Wastes and Frosthollow each claim their variant is older.

[Trans. from Urfic] The original is not written in any alphabet. It is scored in howl-notation: marks indicating pitch, duration, and breath. The translation below is approximate. Some tones have no equivalent in spoken language.

Where the transcription is uncertain, I have marked it [Doubtful]. Where multiple versions exist, I have noted the variance.

This is not scripture. This is what the survivors remembered.

--- Sister Heartwood-Keep

CANTO I: THE SILENCE BEFORE

Urfic Howl-Notation:

Urf-marur. Urf-marur.
A-O-U-E-I-AE-Y.
Vox-urfa marur-se.
Korr-urfa vox-aet.
Aeterna-vox urfa-korr.
Marur-se vox-korr.
Urf-vox aeterna-se.

Interlinear Translation:

Primal-silence. Primal-silence.
A-O-U-E-I-AE-Y.
Howl-primal silence-from.
Stone-world howl-eternal.
Eternal-howl primal-stone.
Silence-from howl-stone.
Primal-howl eternal-from.

Smooth Translation:

From the silence, the primal howl.
A, O, U, E, I, AE, Y.
From the silence, howl and stone.
Stone-world, eternal howl.
Eternal howl, primal stone.
From silence, howl and stone.
From the eternal, the primal howl.

Notes: The hymn is circular. It begins and ends with the primal howl. The seven sacred vowels are listed in the order the moons woke: Emberhowl first, Palehowl last. The text is deliberately repetitive, reflecting the belief that truth is not complex. It is simply always true.

[Doubtful] Some scholars argue the original order of vowels was different. The Ember Wastes variant places Y (Palehowl) first, suggesting the possibility moon was the true catalyst. The Violet Highlands dismiss this as heresy.

CANTO II: THE GRAY ONE'S DESCENT

Urfic Howl-Notation:

Urf-vel korr-marur.
Vel-se korr-urfa.
Urf-vel aeterna-korr.
A-O-U-E-I-AE-Y.
Vox-marur urfa-se.

Interlinear Translation:

Primal-one stone-silence.
One-from stone-primal.
Primal-one eternal-stone.
A-O-U-E-I-AE-Y.
Howl-silence primal-from.

Smooth Translation:

The Primal One walked through silent stone.
From the stone, the Primal One emerged.
The Primal One found eternal stone.
A, O, U, E, I, AE, Y.
From the silence, the first howl.

Notes: This canto describes the Gray Dog's arrival. [Pack Memory] Some versions add a line about "round eyes that caught the stars," which may refer to his glasses. [Doubtful] The Scribepack removed this line as "too recent an addition." Glasses are not mentioned in any text older than 600 YFH.

CANTO III: THE MOONS WAKE

Urfic Howl-Notation:

A-korr ember-se. Ember-vox urfa-korr.
O-korr solar-se. Solar-vox urfa-korr.
U-korr deep-se. Deep-vox urfa-korr.
E-korr green-se. Green-vox urfa-korr.
I-korr umbr-se. Umbr-vox urfa-korr.
AE-korr rose-se. Rose-vox urfa-korr.
Y-korr pale-se. Pale-vox urfa-korr.

Interlinear Translation:

A-stone ember-from. Ember-howl primal-stone.
O-stone solar-from. Solar-howl primal-stone.
U-stone deep-from. Deep-howl primal-stone.
E-stone green-from. Green-howl primal-stone.
I-stone umbr-from. Umbr-howl primal-stone.
AE-stone rose-from. Rose-howl primal-stone.
Y-stone pale-from. Pale-howl primal-stone.

Smooth Translation:

From stone, Emberhowl woke. Emberhowl howled through primal stone.
From stone, Solaris woke. Solaris howled through primal stone.
From stone, Deepwell woke. Deepwell howled through primal stone.
From stone, Evergreen woke. Evergreen howled through primal stone.
From stone, Umbra woke. Umbra howled through primal stone.
From stone, Roseglow woke. Roseglow howled through primal stone.
From stone, Palehowl woke. Palehowl howled through primal stone.

Notes: Each moon is described as waking from stone. This reflects the pre-Howl belief that the moons were Silent Gods made of rock, not light. [Doubtful] The Arcane Order claims the moons were always frequencies, and "stone" is metaphorical. The Wild Order insists it is literal: the moons were once part of the world and were thrown into the sky by the force of the First Howl.

CANTO IV: THE CURRENT FLOWS

Urfic Howl-Notation:

Vox-korr urfa-se.
Current-vox urfa-korr.
Pack-urfa current-se.
Loyalty-vox pack-korr.
A-O-U-E-I-AE-Y.
Urf-marur. Urf-marur.

Interlinear Translation:

Howl-stone primal-from.
Current-howl primal-stone.
Pack-primal current-from.
Loyalty-howl pack-stone.
A-O-U-E-I-AE-Y.
Primal-silence. Primal-silence.

Smooth Translation:

From primal stone, the howl became stone.
From primal stone, the Current howled.
From the Current, the first pack was born.
From the pack, loyalty howled through stone.
A, O, U, E, I, AE, Y.
Primal silence. Primal silence.

Notes: The hymn ends where it began: in silence. The Current is described as a howl that became stone that became pack that became loyalty. This is the theological basis for the Loyalty Network: all Wizards are connected because they share the same primal howl.

[Pack Memory] The Dream Order sings a fifth canto in secret, describing the Second Howl and the falling hats. The Scribepack did not include it in the official transcription, considering it "too recent" to share a hymn with the First Howl. The Flame Order has threatened to burn any dog caught singing it during the Concordance.

THEOLOGICAL DEBATES

The Origin of the Gray Dog

The hymn does not say where the Gray Dog came from. It simply says he "walked through silent stone." Four interpretations dominate:

1. The Emergence Theory: The Gray Dog was not a dog at all. He was silence that became aware of itself. The First Howl was silence learning to make noise. 2. The Traveler Theory: The Gray Dog came from another world, one where magic had already failed. He was searching for a species that could hold it without breaking. He found dogs. 3. The Natural Theory: The Gray Dog was an ordinary dog who sat on the unnamed mountain longer than any other dog had ever sat anywhere. Patience became purpose. Purpose became power. 4. The Hollow Theory: The Gray Dog came from the space between the moons — from the Hollow. He stole sound from silence. The Hollow has been reaching back ever since. (See: The Mute Doctrine.)

The Chorus (the pro-moon orthodoxy) officially supports the Natural Theory. The Mute supports the Hollow Theory. The Arcane Order keeps a private file on all four and refuses to commit.

The Status of the Hymn

Is the hymn divine? The Chorus says yes — it is the literal sound of the First Howl, preserved through the Loyalty Network across four hundred years of oral transmission. The Mute says no — it is a lullaby invented by the first Wizards to justify their power. The Scribepack says both are partly true: the hymn contains genuine pre-Howl elements, but every generation added flourishes until the original is impossible to separate from the ornament.

IN THE ORDERS

Flame

The hymn is sung at the Forge Spire every dawn. The Flame Wizards strike their anvils in time with the vowels, believing that the rhythm of the hymn strengthens their fires.

Radiant

The Radiant Order considers the hymn "incomplete." They believe a sixth canto exists, describing the Gray Dog's glasses as lenses that focused the moonlight. They have been searching for it for three centuries.

Deep

The Tidewatch maintains the oldest written copy, scratched onto obsidian tablets from the Ember Wastes. The Deep Wizards use the hymn for scrying, believing that the vowels reveal futures when chanted underwater.

Wild

The Heartwood Spire sings the hymn in rounds, each Order member taking one vowel. They believe this practice strengthens the roots of the Heartwood tree itself.

Arcane

The Violet Citadel has seventeen competing translations. The Arcane Wizards debate which is most accurate rather than singing any of them.

Dream

The Dream Spire sings the hymn while sleeping. They claim to receive visions of the Gray Dog's dreams when they do.

The Wanderer

When asked about the hymn, the Gray Dog — who can speak when he chooses — reportedly said: "It is close enough." He has never sung it himself.
