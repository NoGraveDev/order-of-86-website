VIII. THE GREAT ROT

A spreading patch of decay and corruption, dark tendrils extending outward, sickly green and black coloration, organic corruption that resists all magic. The existential threat to Pawtheon. Deathforge (Flame) and Blotbark (Wild) are the primary defenders against it.

IX. MAJOR EVENTS & TIMELINE

The Dawn Era

  • The Silence Before: No Wizards, no Towers, no Orders. Only ordinary dogs running in packs beneath seven dormant moons.
  • The Arrival: A silver wolf the size of mountains descends from beyond. It searches for where magic truly belongs.
  • The First Howl: The silver wolf howles. The seven moons wake. Emberhowl, Solaris, Deepwell, Evergreen, Umbra, Roseglow, and Palehowl begin to sing. The Lunar Convergence floods Pawtheon with magic.
  • The Changes: Over generations, dogs grow larger, stand on two legs, gain dexterous paws, expanded minds. They feel the Current but cannot yet shape it with discipline.

The Age of the Hats

  • The Second Howl: The gray dog (later the Wanderer) climbs to the highest peak and howls again. Eighty-six pointed wizard hats drift down from the sky like autumn leaves. They choose their wearers.
  • The Sorting: 18 Flame, 12 Radiant, 13 Deep, 18 Wild, 14 Arcane, 11 Dream. One purple hat descends last to the gray dog himself, with a bent brim pointing upward, pulsing with Palehowl's light.
  • The Crystal Lizards Wake: Small scaly creatures stir in the soil, dormant since the First Howl. Their shed scales hold Pack Magic.
  • Moon Shards Discovered: Crystallized moonlight from the Convergence, dormant until now. Each glows with its moon's color and amplifies magic.

The Tower Age

  • The Six Spires Rise: Each Order builds its tower. Forge Spire in black iron. Solar Spire in white stone and golden glass. Tidewatch inverted into the earth. Heartwood Spire grown from living wood. Violet Citadel in crystal and obsidian. Dream Spire in soft pink stone in the Shadowmire.
  • The Wanderer Refuses: The gray dog builds no spire. He walks between the six towers, teaching and learning, becoming the first Archmage. He refuses leadership of the 86. He refuses membership in the Arcane Order despite his purple hat.

The Loyalty Wars

  • Pre-War Tensions: Rising conflict between Orders. Commonflame (Flame) walks dangerous streets during tensions. Whiteguard (Deep) prevents assassination attempts.
  • The Wars Themselves: Major conflict between Orders. Post-war reconstruction requires Roseheart (Flame) using controlled fire to clear debris. Sleepyheart (Arcane, assisting Dream healers) maintains pack bonds between traumatized Wizards who cannot cooperate while awake.
  • The Great Silence: All magic dies for forty-nine hours. Dogs learn to find heat in movement and purpose. Graystorm (Wild) keeps food plants alive through magical darkness. Blizzardflame (Flame) discovers that the hottest flame comes from the coldest heart. Voidstrike (Flame) maps dead bonds in the Loyalty Network afterward.

Current Era

  • The Great Rot Threatens: Corruption spreads. Blotbark and Deathforge serve as first line of defense. The Rot resists all conventional magic.
  • The Septenary Alignment: A rare conjunction when all seven moons are full. During this time, Palehowl blazes brightest, and common magic becomes supreme.
  • The Wanderer Still Walks: He tells stories, especially his own. He sits patiently where patience is needed. When he howls late at night during moon alignment, every dog in Pawtheon stops and listens.

XI. CULTURE & SOCIETY

  • Crystal Lizard Scales: Each scale holds Pack Magic. When struck, they ring in the seven tones of the original howl.
  • Breed Roles: Each realm has a native breed (Husky/Frosthollow, Tiger/Ember Wastes, Solid/Sunward Heights, Collie/Deepwood, Split/Abyssal Reaches, Classic/Violet Highlands, Zombie/Shadowmire).
  • The Sorting Ceremony: Event where young dogs are chosen by hats. Not all are chosen; only 86 Wizards exist.
  • Political Structures: Range from the Violet Highlands' Grand Assembly to the Ember Wastes' solitary independence to the Abyssal Reaches' Twin Council.
  • Architecture: Varies by realm, from the grown wood of Deepwood to the shifting stone of Shadowmire to the pure meadows of Violet Highlands (open meadows surrounding established settlements).
  • Art Style: 1950s hand-drawn cel animation, thick black outlines, flat colors, limited 8-16 color palettes, Fleischer/UPA/Disney influence.
  • Celestial Aesthetic: All seven moons are distinct non-spherical shapes (bulbous, oblate, ellipsoid, lumpy, faceted, crescent, pockmarked), inspired by Saturn's moons.
