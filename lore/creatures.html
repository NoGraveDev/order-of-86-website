CREATURES OF PAWTHEON

The Expanded Bestiary of the Order of 86

All creatures rendered in 1950s hand-drawn cartoon animation style. Thick confident black ink outlines, flat cel-painted colors, limited 8-12 color palettes, no gradients, no photorealism. Type A (transparent background PNG) or Type B (full scene illustration).

HOW TO USE THIS BESTIARY

Each entry includes:

  • Visual Summary: Breed, size, color palette, and distinctive silhouette
  • Realm / Affiliation: Where it lives or which Order claims it
  • Behavior: How it acts in the world
  • Lore Hook: Why it matters to the Order of 86
  • Prompt Notes: Key visual descriptors for art generation

Total creatures in this expansion: 96 unique beings

CATEGORY 1: REALM-SPECIFIC FAUNA (35 Creatures)

Native animals evolved in (or were shaped by) each realm's unique environment. Not inherently magical, but deeply attuned to their home.

FROSTHOLLOW FAUNA (5)

1. Aurora Fox A small arctic fox whose fur shifts through violet, green, and pale blue in rippling waves, mimicking the aurora. Not magical; the fur simply refracts ambient aurora light. Sits perfectly still for hours, then darts in blur-fast bursts.

  • Palette: Arctic white, aurora violet, aurora green, ice blue, black ink
  • Silhouette: Triangular ears, huge fluffy tail, compact body
  • Lore: Frosthollow children believe seeing an Aurora Fox means the weather will change within the hour. Frostbark has one as a companion.

2. Sastrugi Hare A snow hare whose fur grows in sharp, wind-sculpted ridges identical to the sastrugi snow formations. When hunkered down, it is invisible against the drifts. Leaves no tracks; it bounds from ridge to ridge.

  • Palette: Snow white, slate shadow, ice-blue ear tips, black ink
  • Silhouette: Sharp angular fur ridges, long ears with notched tips
  • Lore: The Huskies of Frosthollow use Sastrugi Hare pelts for camouflage cloaks. Vanguard's visor was originally designed to spot them.

3. Echo Bat A tiny pale bat with translucent wing membranes that preserve sounds like the Frozen Lake preserves echoes. If you hold one to your ear, you hear random snippets of conversations from hours ago.

  • Palette: Pale gray, translucent wing pink, ice blue, black ink
  • Silhouette: Round body, oversized ears, delicate wing fingers
  • Lore: The Ice Hall uses Echo Bats as messengers. Their wings carry spoken words across distances.

4. Glacial Carp A fish that swims beneath the ice sheets of Frosthollow's frozen lakes. Its scales are literally frozen, chip-like and crystalline, yet it moves with fluid grace. When caught and eaten, the scales melt into fresh water.

  • Palette: Ice blue, crystalline white, silver flash, black ink
  • Silhouette: Flat, wide, with rigid frozen fins
  • Lore: The only food source available during the Great Silence. Frostbark's arctic agriculture was inspired by studying how Glacial Carp survive under ice.

5. Ice Mole A blind mole with claws of solid ice-hard crystal. It tunnels through glaciers as easily as a common mole tunnels through soil. Its whiskers detect temperature changes across vast distances.

  • Palette: White fur, crystal claw silver, pink nose, black ink
  • Silhouette: Cylindrical body, huge crystal front paws, no visible eyes
  • Lore: Ice Mole tunnels form natural ventilation in the Ice Hall. Some tunnels lead to places no dog has ever seen.

EMBER WASTES FAUNA (5)

6. Ash Rabbit A rabbit with fur like gray ash that never washes clean. Its eyes glow a faint ember-orange from living near lava cracks. Fireproof to the point of being warm to the touch.

  • Palette: Ash gray, ember orange eyes, charcoal ears, black ink
  • Silhouette: Compact, round, constantly twitching nose
  • Lore: Ash Rabbit warrens are the first warning sign of a new lava flow. When they flee upward, the ground is about to crack.

7. Magma Crab A crab the size of a dog's paw with a shell of cooled black basalt. It dips its claws into lava to reheat them, then uses the superheated pincers to crack open fireproof nuts. Friendly, almost domesticated.

  • Palette: Basalt black, lava-orange claw glow, coal gray, black ink
  • Silhouette: Low wide dome shell, asymmetrical claws, stubby legs
  • Lore: Flame Order apprentices keep Magma Crabs as forge pets. They'll warm a cold tool in a pinch.

8. Cinder Swallow A bird seemingly made of soot and cinder. If it is killed or crushed, it dissolves into ash... and reforms from any nearby fire within hours. Cannot exist outside the Ember Wastes; the air is too cold elsewhere.

  • Palette: Soot black, ash gray, ember red wing tips, black ink
  • Silhouette: Streamlined dart-shape, forked tail, constantly shedding small particles
  • Lore: The Cinder Swallow is the Ember Wastes' answer to immortality. Lensfire has tried to study their reforming process but cannot replicate it.

9. Pumice Tortoise A tortoise with a shell of porous pumice stone, light enough to float on lava. It drifts on magma pools like a living boat, grazing on heat-resistant algae. Dogs sometimes ride them across short lava flows.

  • Palette: Pumice gray, lava-algae green, warm stone tan, black ink
  • Silhouette: Dome shell with visible pores, stubby legs, calm expression
  • Lore: Tideforge once used a Pumice Tortoise to cross a lava channel while forging underwater fire tools.

10. Sulfur Moth A yellow moth that leaves trails of powdered sulfur wherever it flies. Its wings are slightly phosphorescent at night. The powder is harmless but smells strongly of rotten eggs.

  • Palette: Sulfur yellow, phosphor green wing dust, charcoal body, black ink
  • Silhouette: Broad triangular wings, fuzzy body, powder drifting behind
  • Lore: Sulfur Moth powder is a key ingredient in forge-fire starters. The Flame Order cultivates them near the Forge Spire.

SUNWARD HEIGHTS FAUNA (5)

11. Light Finch A small songbird whose feathers glow softly with stored sunlight. At night, a flock of Light Finches can illuminate a field. Their song is beautiful but only audible during daylight hours.

  • Palette: Sun-yellow, warm white glow, pale gold, black ink
  • Silhouette: Round puffed chest, short tail, bright beak
  • Lore: Goldforge keeps a cage of Light Finches that provide reading light during solar storms. They glow brightest when he is most powerful.

12. Pollen Bear A bear the size of a large dog, covered in thick golden fur that collects luminous pollen from the glowing grass. Shakes clouds of golden dust when startled. Gentle unless provoked.

  • Palette: Honey gold, pollen yellow, warm brown paws, black ink
  • Silhouette: Rounded, shaggy, tiny ears, broad paws
  • Lore: Pollen Bear honey is the sweetest substance in Pawtheon. Goldstrike once rescued a cub from a rockslide and was gifted a year's supply.

13. Mirage Hare A hare that naturally creates mirror-images of itself through light refraction in its fur. When chased, a pack of hounds might pursue three identical hares, all of them real, none of them the original.

  • Palette: Pale gold, white underbelly, sun-bleached tan, black ink
  • Silhouette: Lean, long-eared, always slightly blurred at the edges
  • Lore: Mirage Hare fur was once used to make cloaks of illusion. The practice was banned after a diplomatic incident in the Violet Highlands.

14. Sunstone Beetle A beetle with a shell of transparent crystal that focuses sunlight into a beam hot enough to start fires. Rolls the crystal shell open and closed to regulate the beam.

  • Palette: Crystal clear, focused beam yellow-orange, dark body, black ink
  • Silhouette: Domed crystal shell, six legs, antennae like tuning forks
  • Lore: Sunstone Beetles taught the Radiant Order the principles of focused light magic. Their shells are studied but never harvested.

15. Mothpillar The larval form of the Moon Moth. A fat, fuzzy caterpillar that eats only luminous grass and stores moonlight in its body. When it pupates, the chrysalis glows for seven days before hatching.

  • Palette: Leaf green, moon-white spots, fuzzy yellow, black ink
  • Silhouette: Segmented, many-legged, round and soft
  • Lore: The Dream Order believes Mothpillars dream while awake, making them naturally attuned to oneiroic energy. Sleepyheart keeps several as study subjects.

DEEPWOOD FAUNA (5)

16. Moss Badger A badger whose black-and-white stripes are overgrown with bioluminescent green moss. The moss glows brighter when the badger is agitated. Digs tunnels that become nutrient highways for tree roots.

  • Palette: Badger black, badger white, glow-moss green, earth brown, black ink
  • Silhouette: Low, broad, powerful shoulders, glowing stripes
  • Lore: Greensight tracks Moss Badger tunnels to map the forest's root network. The badgers are unwitting gardeners of the Deepwood.

17. Root Snake A snake that travels through the soil by swimming along tree root networks rather than digging. Its scales are bark-brown and leaf-green. Non-venomous; eats insects that damage roots.

  • Palette: Bark brown, leaf green belly, root tan, black ink
  • Silhouette: Long, thin, perfectly cylindrical, no visible neck
  • Lore: Root Snakes are the Deepwood's immune system. Where they gather in numbers, the forest is healthy. Where they flee, the Great Rot may be coming.

18. Spore Fox A fox with a tail that releases harmless bioluminescent spores when wagged. The spores grow into tiny glowing mushrooms wherever they land. The fox itself is ordinary; the spores are symbiotic fungi.

  • Palette: Rust red, white chest, spore glow green, black ink
  • Silhouette: Bushy spore-cloud tail, pointed ears, curious tilted head
  • Lore: Spore Fox trails mark safe paths through the Deepwood at night. Chaosbloom has cultivated the spores into accelerated-evolution gardens.

19. Bark Owl (Deepwood) Not a Wisdom Owl. A small predatory owl whose feathers perfectly mimic tree bark texture. So still it looks like a knot. Eats Shadow Mites before they can damage magical bonds.

  • Palette: Bark brown, knot-gray, amber hunting eyes, black ink
  • Silhouette: Compact, flat-faced, feather tufts like broken branches
  • Lore: The Wild Order encourages Bark Owls to nest in the Heartwood Spire. They are natural pest control against the Rot's insect vectors.

20. Stream Salamander A silver-blue salamander that lives only in the silver streams of the Deepwood. Its skin is cool and smooth, and it can regenerate any lost limb within a day.

  • Palette: Silver blue, stream-white belly, dark spot pattern, black ink
  • Silhouette: Long body, four stubby legs, flat paddle tail
  • Lore: Stream Salamander regeneration inspired early Wild healing magic. Stripeheart's beast sanctuary has a protected stream where they thrive.

ABYSSAL REACHES FAUNA (5)

21. Tide Crab A crab with one massive claw adapted for crushing shells underwater and one smaller, delicate claw for handling objects on land. Changes shell color based on whether the tide is in or out.

  • Palette: Ocean blue (high tide), sand tan (low tide), coral red joints, black ink
  • Silhouette: Asymmetrical claws, wide flat body, sideways walk
  • Lore: Tide Crabs are the unofficial symbol of the Abyssal Reaches' Twin Council. Their dual nature mirrors the realm's divided politics.

22. Spray Seal A translucent seal seemingly made of sea foam and salt spray. It has no solid body; you can put your paw through it. Yet it barks, it hunts, it raises pups on rocky shores.

  • Palette: Sea foam white, spray transparent blue, salt crystal white, black ink
  • Silhouette: Blobby, ever-shifting, vaguely seal-shaped
  • Lore: Spray Seals are believed to be the Abyssal Reaches' answer to the Current; creatures made of water magic that achieved independence. Mixedsight studies them for paradox prophecy.

23. Whirlpool Eel An eel that swims in tight circles, creating miniature whirlpools that trap prey. Its body is striped in churning white and deep blue. Can create a whirlpool large enough to drown a dog if agitated.

  • Palette: Churn white, deep blue, electric stripe yellow, black ink
  • Silhouette: Long serpentine, pointed snout, constantly coiling
  • Lore: Whirlpool Eels are the reason the Abyssal Reaches built the Divide. Before the cliff, they swam upstream and flooded villages.

24. Coral Hound A dog-sized creature made of living coral, shaped by centuries of gradual growth into a quadruped form. It has no mind, but it follows dogs loyally, mimicking their movements. Friendly but scratchy.

  • Palette: Coral pink, reef orange, algae green patches, black ink
  • Silhouette: Dog-shaped but with visible coral branching, rough texture
  • Lore: Coral Hounds are the Abyssal Reaches' attempt at creating life without magic. Tideseed has tried transplanting them to tidal forests, with mixed success.

25. Brine Fly A fly that leaves behind perfect salt crystals everywhere it lands. Its wings are iridescent and fragile. Swarms of Brine Flies can encrust an entire dock in salt overnight.

  • Palette: Iridescent wing rainbow, salt white, body black, black ink
  • Silhouette: Tiny, two wings, long legs, crystal residue trailing
  • Lore: Brine Fly salt is prized by the Flame Order for preserving forge materials. The Abyssal Reaches export it as a trade good.

VIOLET HIGHLANDS FAUNA (5)

26. Meadow Lark A songbird with pale violet feathers that sings in complex harmonics. Its song is different for every listener; some hear a lullaby, others a call to action. Nests in the tall violet wildflowers.

  • Palette: Violet feather, lavender wing, cream belly, black ink
  • Silhouette: Plump, short tail, open beak, musical note shapes nearby
  • Lore: The Grand Assembly once tried to use Meadow Larks to communicate secret votes. The birds sang different results to different dogs, so the plan was abandoned.

27. Scroll Mouse A tiny mouse that builds its nest from torn parchment and ink-stained scraps. Its paws are permanently black with ink. It can read simple words and often steals important documents for nesting material.

  • Palette: Parchment tan, ink black paws, pink ears, black ink
  • Silhouette: Tiny, round, black-footed, carrying a paper scrap
  • Lore: The Archivum employs Scroll Mouse catchers. Grayglasses once found a lost prophecy in a Scroll Mouse nest.

28. Lavender Moth A moth with wings the exact shade of violet Highland wildflowers. It pollinates exclusively lavender and violet blooms. Its caterpillar form is prized as a scholarly pet.

  • Palette: Lavender wing, violet body, white spot eyes, black ink
  • Silhouette: Broad rounded wings, fuzzy body, delicate antennae
  • Lore: Lavender Moth silk is used for the collars and scarves of Violet Highlands Wizards. It never stains and always smells faintly of flowers.

29. Archive Beetle A beetle that eats only old parchment and paper. Its shell is patterned like illegible handwriting. If you crush one, the dust smells like a library. Sometimes knows secrets it ate.

  • Palette: Parchment yellow, ink-black scribble pattern, brown legs, black ink
  • Silhouette: Dome shell with text-like markings, small legs, mandibles
  • Lore: Archive Beetles are both pests and preservationists. They eat rotting paper before mold can spread, but also devour priceless documents.

30. Quartz Finch A finch that builds its nest entirely from quartz crystal fragments and shiny stones. The nest is so heavy it can only be built in low shrubs. The eggs inside are pale blue and cold to the touch.

  • Palette: Finch brown, quartz clear/white, pale blue egg, black ink
  • Silhouette: Small bird, glittering nest base, sharp beak
  • Lore: Quartz Finch nests are considered good luck by students at the Violet Citadel. Finding one before an exam is said to guarantee success.

SHADOWMIRE FAUNA (5)

31. Mist Cat A cat seemingly made of white-gray fog. It has no solid form; wind distorts its shape. It can pass through gaps smaller than a paw. Purrs loudly but leaves damp paw prints.

  • Palette: Mist white, fog gray, shadow dark gray, yellow eyes, black ink
  • Silhouette: Cat-shaped but constantly shifting, semi-transparent
  • Lore: Mist Cats are drawn to the Dream Spire. Whitemist believes they are dreams that escaped into waking reality.

32. Rot Puppy A small dog, once ordinary, partially corrupted by the Great Rot. Patches of gray-green rot on its fur, but its tail still wags. Still loyal. Still wants to play. The saddest creature in Pawtheon.

  • Palette: Normal fur color, rot green patches, sad brown eyes, black ink
  • Silhouette: Small dog, patchy fur, one ear up one ear down
  • Lore: Rot Puppies are why the Wild Order fights. Deathbloom has saved seventeen so far, curing them through Necromantic Botany. Grimbloom tends the ones still waiting.

33. Bog Lantern A floating sphere of cold blue-green light that drifts through the Shadowmire mists. It has no body, only light. It leads lost travelers toward deep water. Not evil; simply hungry for company.

  • Palette: Cold blue, sickly green, darkness black, black ink
  • Silhouette: Sphere with flickering tendrils of light, no solid form
  • Lore: Bog Lanterns are the trapped oneiroic energy of dogs who died alone in the mists. The Dream Order is researching how to free them.

34. Ash Raven A raven with feathers like charred paper, black and gray and fragile. When it flies, small flakes drift off behind it. Its call sounds like a fire crackling.

  • Palette: Ash gray, char black, ember red eye, black ink
  • Silhouette: Sharp beak, tattered feathers, long tail
  • Lore: Ash Ravens appear wherever the Great Rot has burned itself out. Their presence means the corruption has passed... but so has everything else.

35. Ooze Hare A hare partially melted by the Shifting Grounds. Its fur is slimy, its legs uneven lengths. It hops in lopsided arcs, leaving a trail of harmless but foul-smelling slime.

  • Palette: Mud brown, slime green, patchy fur pink, black ink
  • Silhouette: Lopsided, one ear longer, dripping, sad eyes
  • Lore: Ooze Hares are what happens when the Shifting Grounds transform a creature mid-step. Greenbone studies them to understand chaos transformation limits.

STARTER LANDS FAUNA (5)

36. Prairie Dog The original, pre-magic dog ancestor. Small, brown, unremarkable, lives in underground colonies. No magic whatsoever. The template from which all evolved breeds emerged.

  • Palette: Earth brown, tan belly, dark eye, black ink
  • Silhouette: Short, round, upright alert posture, tiny paws
  • Lore: The Wanderer visits Prairie Dog colonies regularly. He says they remind him what dogs were before magic made them complicated.

37. Field Mouse The most common mouse in Pawtheon. Brown, tiny, harmless. Every realm has them, but the Starter Lands field mice are the originals. They outnumber dogs a thousand to one.

  • Palette: Mouse brown, pink tail, black eye, black ink
  • Silhouette: Tiny, pointed nose, round ears, thin tail
  • Lore: Field mice are invisible to prophecy. No Deep Wizard has ever successfully predicted what a field mouse will do. Tanwatcher finds this fascinating.

38. Stone Chipmunk A chipmunk obsessed with collecting interesting rocks. Its cheek pouches can hold stones twice the size of its head. Builds cairns at the entrances to its burrows.

  • Palette: Chipmunk stripe brown, stone gray, pink paws, black ink
  • Silhouette: Puffed cheeks, striped back, tiny busy paws
  • Lore: Stone Chipmunk cairns are the first markers young dogs learn to read in the Starter Lands. Each cairn points toward the nearest water source.

39. Grass Snake A slender green-gold snake that lives in the tall grass. Completely harmless; eats insects. Its scales match the grass so perfectly that dogs step on it constantly. It never bites.

  • Palette: Grass green, gold stripe, pale belly, black ink
  • Silhouette: Long, thin, perfectly straight when still
  • Lore: Grass Snakes are considered good omens. If one crosses your path, the Wanderer says, it means the Current is flowing smoothly beneath your paws.

40. Dawn Robin A red-breasted bird that sings exactly at dawn, never before, never after. Its call is the same seven-note melody across all of Pawtheon. Some say it echoes the First Howl.

  • Palette: Robin red breast, brown back, cream belly, black ink
  • Silhouette: Round body, upright posture, open beak at sunrise
  • Lore: The Dawn Robin's song is the closest non-magical sound to the original seven tones. Musicians in the Violet Highlands have tried to transcribe it for centuries.

CATEGORY 2: MOON-TOUCHED BEASTS (14 Creatures)

Creatures directly altered by prolonged exposure to a specific moon's light. More magical than realm fauna; each carries a fragment of lunar resonance.

41. Ember Wolf A wolf with fur like cooling lava, black basalt base with cracks of glowing orange. Only appears during Emberhowl's full phase. Its howl sounds like flames consuming dry wood. Carnivorous but avoids dogs.

  • Palette: Basalt black, magma orange cracks, ash gray, black ink
  • Moon: Emberhowl
  • Lore: The Flame Order believes Ember Wolves are the original wolves from which the First Howl was born, transformed by centuries of moonlight.

42. Solar Stag A stag whose antlers are solid concentrated light, too bright to look at directly. Its coat is pure white-gold. It casts no shadow. If killed, its antlers dim but do not fade for seven days.

  • Palette: White-gold, blinding antler yellow, sun white, black ink
  • Moon: Solaris
  • Lore: The Radiant Order hunts the Solar Stag once per cycle. Not to kill; to follow. Where it leads, truth is revealed.

43. Well Whale A whale the size of a small hill that does not swim in water but through solid earth. Its passage leaves behind deep trenches that fill with groundwater. Its song is felt in the paws, not heard.

  • Palette: Ocean blue, trench black, bioluminescent spot blue, black ink
  • Moon: Deepwell
  • Lore: Deepwell Wizards believe the Well Whale's path predicts future water sources. Bernardguard followed one to find the Truth Mirror's original location.

44. Evergreen Bear A massive bear that hibernates inside living tree trunks, merging with the wood. When it emerges, moss and small flowers grow from its fur. It protects forest boundaries fiercely.

  • Palette: Bark brown, moss green, flower pink, black ink
  • Moon: Evergreen
  • Lore: The Wild Order considers Evergreen Bears sacred. To harm one is to invite the forest's wrath. Brownthorn has befriended one that guards the Heartwood Spire's eastern border.

45. Umbra Cat A cat that phases in and out of shadow unpredictably. Its fur is marked with shifting geometric patterns like Umbra's plates. It can walk through walls if they are thick enough to cast a shadow.

  • Palette: Shadow black, geometric pattern violet, star white spot, black ink
  • Moon: Umbra
  • Lore: Umbra Cats are drawn to the Violet Citadel's restricted wing. They know secret passages that even the Arcane Order has forgotten.

46. Dream Hare A hare that sleeps with its eyes open and walks in the dream realm while its body remains still. If you touch a sleeping Dream Hare, you experience fragments of its dream.

  • Palette: Soft pink, lavender spots, cloud white belly, black ink
  • Moon: Roseglow
  • Lore: The Dream Order harvests nothing from Dream Hares, but observes them constantly. Their dreams are said to contain prophecies too subtle for Deep Wizards to interpret.

47. Pale Mouse The smallest mammal in Pawtheon, nearly invisible against any background. It is considered the luckiest creature to encounter. Even dogs who hate mice spare the Pale Mouse.

  • Palette: Pale silver, faint blue-white, tiny pink ears, black ink
  • Moon: Palehowl
  • Lore: The Wanderer always carries a dried Pale Mouse whisker in his fur. He says it was a gift from the first one, before the moons sang.

48. Convergence Moth A rare moth that only appears during the Septenary Alignment. Its wings display all seven moon colors simultaneously. It lives for only one night, lays eggs, and dies. The eggs remain dormant until the next true Septenary Alignment,495civil years later.

  • Palette: All seven moon colors in bands, white body, black ink
  • Moon: Septenary Alignment
  • Lore: To see a Convergence Moth is the rarest omen. Every Order interprets it differently. The Flame sees hope. The Deep sees endings. The Dream sees new beginnings.

49. Eclipse Bat A bat that only flies when two or more moons share the sky simultaneously. Its shadow is visible even in total darkness. It feeds on moonlight itself, not insects.

  • Palette: Shadow black, silver moonlight edge, eclipse ring gray, black ink
  • Moon: Multi-lunar events
  • Lore: Eclipse Bats are the Deep Order's favorite scrying companions. Their shadows reveal hidden paths when the light is right.

50. Newmoon Mole A blind mole that becomes active only during the new moon phase, when no lunar light touches Pawtheon. It navigates by sensing the absence of light. Completely invisible in darkness.

  • Palette: Absolute black, faint starlight gray whisker, black ink
  • Moon: New moon (absence)
  • Lore: Newmoon Moles are the only creatures that can find things hidden by lunar magic. The Arcane Order uses them to locate hidden archives.

51. Waxing Fox A fox that grows larger and more powerful as its associated moon waxes, then shrinks and sleeps as it wanes. There are seven subspecies, each tied to a different moon.

  • Palette: Varies by moon association; Emberhowl = orange, Solaris = gold, etc.
  • Moon: All seven (subspecies)
  • Lore: Waxing Foxes are how the Wild Order tracks moon cycles in remote areas. The largest fox indicates which moon is closest to full.

52. Waning Owl An owl that becomes transparent as its moon wanes. At the new moon, it is completely invisible and silent. At the full moon, it is solid and loudly hooting.

  • Palette: Varies by moon; becomes increasingly transparent
  • Moon: All seven (subspecies)
  • Lore: Waning Owls are messengers between realms. They travel safest when invisible, delivering news at the full moon when they can be seen and heard.

53. Tide Runner A deer-like creature whose antlers are made of frozen tidal water from Deepwell. It runs along shorelines at twilight, its antlers leaving trails of salt water that evaporate by morning.

  • Palette: Ocean blue, salt white antler, sand tan, black ink
  • Moon: Deepwell + Solaris
  • Lore: Tide Runners are a sign of good relations between the Deep and Radiant Orders. Their appearance means the tides and the sun are in harmony.

54. Ash Phoenix A bird that immolates itself in controlled flame every dawn and is reborn from its own ashes by sunset. Only found where Emberhowl and Solaris light overlap at dawn.

  • Palette: Flame orange, ash gray, newborn gold, black ink
  • Moon: Emberhowl + Solaris
  • Lore: The Flame and Radiant Orders both claim the Ash Phoenix as their symbol. No treaty has ever resolved this dispute.

CATEGORY 3: ORDER FAMILIARS & SERVITORS (18 Creatures)

Creatures bound to, created by, or closely associated with the six Orders and their Towers. Some are pets. Some are tools. Some are colleagues.

FLAME ORDER FAMILIARS (3)

55. Forge Salamander A fireproof salamander that lives in the coals of the Forge Spire. It is warm to the touch and glows orange from within. Apprentices keep them in pockets to stay warm in the cold forges.

  • Palette: Coal black, forge orange belly, ember red spot, black ink
  • Silhouette: Long body, tiny legs, permanently glowing
  • Lore: Forge Salamanders cannot survive outside the Ember Wastes. Frostforge keeps a special heated box for his, named Cinder.

56. Spark Moth A moth that is attracted to open flame but never burns. Its wings are patterned like ember sparks. When threatened, it releases a cloud of harmless but startling bright sparks.

  • Palette: Ash gray, spark orange wing pattern, ember yellow, black ink
  • Silhouette: Moth shape with jagged wing edges, fuzzy body
  • Lore: Spark Moths are the Forge Spire's alarm system. If they suddenly scatter, a forge has gone unstable.

57. Anvil Beetle A beetle with a shell shaped like a tiny anvil. It is incredibly dense for its size; dropped from a height, it lands with a clang. Used by Flame apprentices to test forge-hardened surfaces.

  • Palette: Iron gray, rust orange, black shell spot, black ink
  • Silhouette: Anvil-shaped dome, six sturdy legs
  • Lore: Anvil Beetles eat only cooling slag. They are essential waste-management at the Forge Spire.

RADIANT ORDER FAMILIARS (3)

58. Gleam Finch A finch that reflects light like a mirror. It cannot generate light, but it can redirect sunlight into dark corners. Trained Radiant Wizards use them to send light signals across distances.

  • Palette: Mirror silver, bright white, sky blue reflection, black ink
  • Silhouette: Small bird, reflective feathers, sharp beak
  • Lore: Gleam Finches are the Solar Spire's communication network. Goldstrike has trained three to follow him and provide light on demand.

59. Halo Moth A moth with a ring of golden light around its body at all times. The halo dims when the moth is frightened. It is drawn to truth-telling and will land on honest dogs.

  • Palette: White wing, gold halo ring, soft yellow body, black ink
  • Silhouette: Moth with visible golden ring, gentle flight
  • Lore: Halo Moths are used in Radiant trials. If a moth refuses to land on a witness, their testimony is suspect.

60. Prism Hare A white hare that separates light into rainbow colors as it runs, leaving brief rainbow trails. Its fur is normal white; the effect comes from magical oils it secretes.

  • Palette: White fur, rainbow trail bands, pink nose, black ink
  • Silhouette: Running hare, rainbow streaks behind
  • Lore: Prism Hare oil is a key ingredient in truth-revealing potions. The Radiant Order breeds them in the Purified Plateau.

DEEP ORDER FAMILIARS (3)

61. Prophetic Squid A small squid that lives in the Tidewatch's still water pool. Its ink forms random patterns that Deep Wizards interpret as prophecies. The ink disappears after one minute.

  • Palette: Squid blue, ink black, bioluminescent spot cyan, black ink
  • Silhouette: Tentacled, floating, releasing ink clouds
  • Lore: Prophetic Squid ink is never written down. The Deep Order believes recording prophecy changes it. Pinkgaze has memorized over 400 squid patterns.

62. Mirror Frog A frog with skin like still water, perfectly reflective. If you look into its back, you see your own face... but older, or younger, or different in some subtle way.

  • Palette: Mirror silver, water blue, ripple gray, black ink
  • Silhouette: Round frog, perfectly reflective back, calm posture
  • Lore: Mirror Frogs are the first test for Deep apprentices. If you cannot handle seeing your own changed reflection, you cannot handle true prophecy.

CATEGORY 4: CURRENT SWIMMERS & PACK MAGIC MANIFESTATIONS (12 Creatures)

Beings formed from the raw Current itself, or from Pack Magic made manifest. They are not born; they emerge.

73. Loyalty Hound A golden dog-shaped being made of pure warm Pack Magic. It appears when a pack's loyalty reaches extraordinary levels. It cannot fight, but its presence amplifies all pack members' magic tenfold.

  • Palette: Warm gold, soft white glow, Pack Magic yellow, black ink
  • Silhouette: Dog-shaped light, no solid features, gently pulsing
  • Lore: A Loyalty Hound appeared during the Loyalty Wars' final battle. Both sides stopped fighting to watch it. It is the only recorded instance of both sides agreeing on anything.

74. Fetch Sprite Already in base directory, expanded here. A small energetic creature of pure Pack Magic shaped like a running dog. Carries tiny objects in its mouth. Playful, loyal, attracted to acts of devotion.

  • Palette: Warm orange, soft yellow, sprite white, playful pink, black ink
  • Silhouette: Running dog made of light, tail wagging blur
  • Lore: Fetch Sprites appear whenever a dog performs a selfless act for their pack. They bring good luck. The more sprites, the stronger the pack's bond.

75. Bond Moth A moth that appears when two dogs form a deep, genuine friendship. It lives as long as the friendship lasts. If the friendship breaks, the moth dies. Its wings display the colors of both friends.

  • Palette: Friendship color mix, silver thread body, glow white, black ink
  • Silhouette: Moth with two-tone wings, gentle glow
  • Lore: The Dream Order tracks Bond Moths to study pack dynamics. The Warlock's presence causes them to multiply wildly.

76. Current Eel An eel that swims through the Current itself, not water. Invisible except as a ripple in the air. It follows ley lines and appears where the Current is strongest.

  • Palette: Current blue, ripple silver, transparent body, black ink
  • Silhouette: Long serpentine ripple in reality, barely visible
  • Lore: Current Eels are how the Arcane Order maps the Current. Where they gather, magic is potent. Where they flee, the Current is disturbed.

77. Echo Wolf The preserved memory of a dead wolf, sustained by the Current and the Loyalty Network. It looks solid but is slightly translucent. It cannot speak but can howl. It remembers its pack.

  • Palette: Ghost gray, faint moonlight silver, transparent edges, black ink
  • Silhouette: Wolf-shaped memory, noble posture, fading at edges
  • Lore: Echo Wolves are honored, not feared. The Wanderer says they are proof that loyalty outlasts death. Some packs keep centuries-old Echo Wolves as honored ancestors.

78. Pack Sprite A tiny being of shifting light that appears when three or more dogs howl in harmony. It dances in the center of the circle, feeding on the sound. When the howl ends, it gifts each singer a small blessing.

  • Palette: Shifting warm colors, harmony rainbow, soft glow, black ink
  • Silhouette: Small dancing light, vaguely canine, joyful
  • Lore: Pack Sprites are the most common Current manifestation. Children are taught to howl in groups specifically to summon them.

79. Resonance Cricket A cricket whose chirp matches the frequency of the Current in its area. In places of strong magic, it chirps constantly. In dead zones, it is silent. It cannot survive away from the Current.

  • Palette: Cricket green, Current-glow yellow, antenna silver, black ink
  • Silhouette: Cricket with glowing abdomen, long antennae
  • Lore: Resonance Crickets are carried by traveling Wizards as magical barometers. A silent cricket means danger.

80. Memory Fish A fish that swims in still water and shows memories in its scales. If you look at it from above, you see moving images in its back. The images are random: a pup's first steps, a battle, a sunset.

  • Palette: Water blue, memory-color shifting scales, silver flash, black ink
  • Silhouette: Flat fish, shifting images visible on back, slow swimmer
  • Lore: Memory Fish are how the Deepwood preserves its history. The oldest Memory Fish is said to remember the First Howl.

81. Loyalty Leech A rare, unwanted manifestation. A leech made of cold Pack Magic that attaches to fractured packs and feeds on their weakened loyalty. It grows larger as the pack fights. Removing it requires genuine reconciliation.

  • Palette: Cold blue, sickly gray, dim glow, black ink
  • Silhouette: Leech shape, segmented, pulsing with stolen light
  • Lore: Loyalty Leeches are the Dream Order's primary concern. They are attracted to trauma and can destroy a pack from within if not addressed.

82. Howling Wind Not a creature but a phenomenon: a wind that carries the sound of distant howling. It follows dogs who are lost or alone, guiding them toward packs. It is the Current's voice.

  • Palette: Wind white, sound wave gray, pale blue, black ink
  • Silhouette: Swirling wind with visible sound waves, directional
  • Lore: The Wanderer can call the Howling Wind by name. It always answers him, no matter where he is.

83. Starlight Moth A moth that drinks starlight instead of nectar. Its wings display accurate star maps. Navigators use them to travel at night. They only fly on clear nights.

  • Palette: Star white, night blue, constellation gold lines, black ink
  • Silhouette: Moth with star-map wings, delicate, nocturnal
  • Lore: Starlight Moths were essential before the towers were built. They were the original guides between realms.

84. Friendship Fern A plant, not an animal, but sentient enough to count. A fern that grows only where two dogs have sworn genuine loyalty to each other. It unfurls new fronds on the anniversary of the oath.

  • Palette: Fern green, oath-gold vein, friendship pink tip, black ink
  • Silhouette: Fern with glowing veins, small, persistent
  • Lore: Friendship Ferns are the Wild Order's record of pack bonds. There is a garden of them at the Heartwood Spire with over 10,000 ferns.

CATEGORY 5: PRE-HOWL ANCIENTS (10 Creatures)

Beings that existed before the First Howl, before magic, before the moons sang. They are old, patient, and mostly indifferent to the Order of 86.

85. Stone Dog A statue of a dog carved from granite, predating the First Howl. It stands perfectly still for years, decades, centuries. Then, without warning, it moves: a single step, a turned head, a blink. Then stillness again.

  • Palette: Granite gray, moss patch green, crack shadow, black ink
  • Silhouette: Dog statue, weathered, mossy, posed mid-stride
  • Lore: The Wanderer claims Stone Dogs are the original dogs, frozen in time, waiting for something. He refuses to say what.

86. Old Fish A fish that has lived since before the moons sang. It is impossibly large, impossibly old, and impossibly wise. It lives in the deepest water of the Abyssal Reaches and never surfaces.

  • Palette: Ancient black, scale silver, eye milky white, black ink
  • Silhouette: Massive fish, heavy scales, small eyes, slow
  • Lore: The Deep Order believes Old Fish remembers the world before magic. No one has ever successfully communicated with it, but it sometimes nudges ships toward safety.

87. First Rabbit The ancestor of all rabbits. Immortal, unafraid, unremarkable. It looks like an ordinary brown rabbit. It has survived every catastrophe in Pawtheon's history by being small, fast, and unimportant.

  • Palette: Earth brown, white tail, black eye, black ink
  • Silhouette: Rabbit, completely ordinary, slightly larger than modern rabbits
  • Lore: The First Rabbit is the only being the Wanderer bows to. He says it was there before him, and it will be there after.

88. Primal Crow A crow that was present for the First Howl. Its feathers hold faint echoes of the seven tones. When it caws, dogs who know what to listen for can hear fragments of the original howl.

  • Palette: Crow black, feather tip silver, ancient eye yellow, black ink
  • Silhouette: Crow, slightly larger than normal, one silver feather
  • Lore: There are only seven Primal Crows, one for each moon. They never die; they simply fly away and are not seen for centuries.

89. Burden Tortoise A tortoise that carries the weight of unspoken promises on its back. The promises manifest as small stones. When a promise is fulfilled, a stone falls off. The tortoise has been growing heavier since the First Howl.

  • Palette: Tortoise brown, promise stone gray, moss green, black ink
  • Silhouette: Tortoise with mountain of small stones on shell, patient
  • Lore: The Dream Order tends to the Burden Tortoise. Every fulfilled promise lightens its load. The Wanderer visits it every year.

90. Silence Worm A worm that burrows through solid rock, leaving tunnels that absorb all sound. The tunnels are completely silent. Dogs who enter them cannot hear their own heartbeats.

  • Palette: Rock gray, tunnel black, pale segment, black ink
  • Silhouette: Segmented worm, rock-dust trail, no visible eyes
  • Lore: Silence Worm tunnels are used for meditation and secret meetings. They predate the First Howl, meaning the world knew silence before it knew sound.

91. Ancient Oak The tree from the Starter Lands. It is not a creature in the animal sense, but it is aware. Its roots touch every realm through the Current. It was there before the First Howl. It will be there after the last.

  • Palette: Oak brown, leaf green-gold, root silver, black ink
  • Silhouette: Massive twisted tree, roots visible, ancient and alone
  • Lore: The Ancient Oak is why the Starter Lands are sacred. The Wanderer sits beneath it when he needs to remember why he walks.

92. Dust Moth A moth made of the dust of ages. It flutters in the oldest places: the Ice Hall, the deepest archive, the Dream Spire's foundation. Where it lands, time moves slightly slower.

  • Palette: Dust tan, age gray, slow-gold, black ink
  • Silhouette: Moth, slightly fuzzy with dust, slow wingbeat
  • Lore: Dust Moths are studied by the Arcane Order for time magic. No one has successfully caught one. They simply dissolve if touched.

93. Hollow Stone A stone with a perfectly round hole through it. The hole was not drilled; it has always been there. Looking through the hole shows the world as it was before the First Howl: dogs on all fours, no magic, silent moons.

  • Palette: Stone gray, hole black, ancient moss, black ink
  • Silhouette: Round stone, perfect hole, weathered surface
  • Lore: There are only three Hollow Stones. The Arcane Order has one. The Wanderer has one. The location of the third is unknown.

94. First Ember A single coal that has been burning since before the First Howl. It is not hot to the touch. It does not consume fuel. It simply burns, small and steady, in a hidden cave in the Ember Wastes.

  • Palette: Coal black, ember red, ash gray, black ink
  • Silhouette: Single coal, small flame, ancient cave setting
  • Lore: The Flame Order guards the First Ember jealously. They believe if it ever goes out, all fire magic will fail. Frostforge has calculated that it will burn for at least 10,000 more years.

CATEGORY 6: THE GREAT ROT & CORRUPTED HORRORS (12 Creatures)

Creatures touched by the Great Rot, corrupted by decay, or born from the absence of Pack Magic. The enemies of the Order.

95. Blight Hawk Already in base directory, expanded. A hawk whose feathers are falling out in patches, revealing rotting skin beneath. It attacks healthy creatures not to eat them, but to spread the Rot.

  • Palette: Rot brown, sickly green, exposed pink skin, black ink
  • Silhouette: Hawk, patchy feathers, mad eyes, tattered wings
  • Lore: Blight Hawks are the Rot's scouts. Where they circle, the Rot will follow. Deathforge burns them from the sky before they can land.

96. Corruption Worm Already in base directory, expanded. A worm that burrows into living things and replaces their insides with rot. The host appears healthy for days, then collapses into decay.

  • Palette: Rot black, sickly green vein, corruption purple, black ink
  • Silhouette: Segmented worm, too many segments, burrowing pose
  • Lore: Corruption Worms are why the Wild Order inspects every tree. Blotbark has developed a root treatment that kills them before they reach the trunk.

97. Despair Deer A deer with drooping antlers and rotting hooves. It does not attack. It simply stands in meadows and radiates sadness. Dogs who see it lose all motivation. The despair is contagious.

  • Palette: Gray fur, rot hoof black, sad eye milky, black ink
  • Silhouette: Deer, head down, antlers drooping, emaciated
  • Lore: Despair Deer are the Rot's psychological weapon. Roseclassic has developed a Radiant technique that purges their influence with warm light.

98. Rot Pup Already in base directory, expanded. A small dog, partially corrupted. Still loyal. Still playful. Still infectious. The most tragic creature in Pawtheon.

  • Palette: Normal fur, rot green patch, sad eye, black ink
  • Silhouette: Small dog, patchy, wagging tail despite sickness
  • Lore: The Dream Order maintains a sanctuary for Rot Pups. Sleepyheart believes their dreams are clean, even if their bodies are not.

99. Hollow Hound Already in base directory, expanded. A dog drained of all emotion and color. Gray fur, empty eyes, tail never wags. The absence of loyalty made visible.

  • Palette: Hollow gray, empty black eye, lifeless dark gray, black ink
  • Silhouette: Dog, sitting unnaturally still, no expression
  • Lore: Hollow Hounds are former pack dogs who lost all connection to the Current. They cannot be cured. They cannot be killed by conventional means. The Flame Order burns them.

100. Loneliness Wraith Already in base directory, expanded. A wispy dog-shaped figure made of cold blue vapor. The spirit of a dog who died without pack. It does not attack; it simply begs for companionship, draining warmth from any who approach.

  • Palette: Wisp blue, mist white, cold shadow, ghost pale, black ink
  • Silhouette: Dog-shaped vapor, sorrowful, trailing tendrils
  • Lore: Loneliness Wraiths are why the Dream Order exists. Every Wizard bonded is one less wraith in the world. The Warlock can absorb them into his dreams, giving them peace.

101. Shadow Mite Already in base directory, expanded. A tiny black insect with multiple legs. It feeds on magical bonds, weakening pack loyalty. Numerous, parasitic, nearly invisible.

  • Palette: Shadow black, mite dark gray, eye red glow, black ink
  • Silhouette: Tiny insect, many legs, glowing red eye
  • Lore: Shadow Mites are the Rot's smallest weapon. They are why pack bonds need constant maintenance. Greensight's root networks detect their presence.

102. Mold Hawk A hawk covered in fuzzy green mold that releases toxic spores when it flies. The spores cause hallucinations of failure and worthlessness. Dogs who breathe too many simply stop moving.

  • Palette: Mold green, spore cloud yellow, rotting feather brown, black ink
  • Silhouette: Hawk, fuzzy with mold, spore trail, tattered
  • Lore: Mold Hawks are drawn to the Violet Citadel's archives. The damp stone is perfect for their spores. The Arcane Order wards against them constantly.

103. Sorrow Crab A crab with a shell made of compressed sadness, heavy and dark. It moves slowly, dragging its shell. Other crabs avoid it. If you touch its shell, you feel grief for things you have not lost.

  • Palette: Grief black, shell dark blue, claw gray, black ink
  • Silhouette: Crab, oversized heavy shell, slow dragging walk
  • Lore: Sorrow Crabs accumulate at the Tidewatch's lowest tier. The Deep Order believes they are drawn to prophetic visions of loss.

104. Doubt Serpent A snake that whispers in dogs' ears, planting questions about their worth, their loyalty, their purpose. The voice is the dog's own. It cannot be seen unless looked at directly.

  • Palette: Whisper gray, shadow black, doubt purple tongue, black ink
  • Silhouette: Serpent, coiled, forked tongue, half-invisible
  • Lore: Doubt Serpents are the Rot's most insidious creation. They target pack leaders. The Dream Order teaches meditation techniques to silence their whispers.

105. Memory Moth A moth that eats memories instead of fabric. It leaves holes in recollection. A dog infested with Memory Moths might forget their own name, their pack, their purpose.

  • Palette: Pale gray, memory-hole black, wing dust silver, black ink
  • Silhouette: Moth, faded colors, tattered wings, hole patterns
  • Lore: Memory Moths are drawn to the Arcane Order's archives. They eat knowledge. Shadowspot's memory-silk is the only material they cannot digest.

106. Fracture Hound A dog whose body is cracked like ice, with green rot glowing in the fissures. It moves in jerks and snaps. Its howl sounds like breaking glass. It was once a loyal pack dog.

  • Palette: Ice gray, fracture line green, broken black, black ink
  • Silhouette: Dog, cracked body, jerky pose, glowing fissures
  • Lore: Fracture Hounds are what happen when Pack Magic is violently stripped away. They cannot be saved. Pureflame believes their existence proves that magic without loyalty is worse than no magic at all.

CATEGORY 7: MISCELLANEOUS MAGICAL BEINGS (8 Creatures)

Oddities, strays, and unique beings that do not fit other categories.

107. The Lost Puppy A small brown puppy that appears at the edge of camps, looking lost and sad. It is not real. It is a manifestation of collective guilt. If a camp takes it in, it disappears by morning, leaving behind a single scale from a Crystal Lizard.

  • Palette: Brown puppy, sad eye, transparent edge, black ink
  • Silhouette: Small puppy, floppy ears, sitting alone
  • Lore: The Lost Puppy appears most often after battles. Both sides see it. No one talks about it.

108. Cartographer Snail A snail with a shell covered in a perfectly accurate map of Pawtheon, updated in real time. If a new road is built, it appears on the shell within a day. The snail moves incredibly slowly.

  • Palette: Shell map colors, snail body tan, trail silver, black ink
  • Silhouette: Snail, shell like a globe, tiny horns
  • Lore: The Arcane Order has tried to breed Cartographer Snails. They refuse to reproduce in captivity. The original lives somewhere in the Violet Highlands.

109. Melody Finch A finch that sings in seven-part harmony by itself. Each note is a different tone, matching the seven moons. Its song is beautiful but slightly out of tune, as if the bird is still learning.

  • Palette: Finch brown, seven-colored throat patch, white belly, black ink
  • Silhouette: Finch, puffed chest, open beak with color notes
  • Lore: Melody Finches appeared after the Second Howl. The Wanderer believes they are trying to recreate the First Howl, one note at a time.

110. Truth Beetle A beetle that changes color when lies are spoken near it. Green for truth. Red for lies. Black for self-deception. It is drawn to political assemblies and courtrooms.

  • Palette: Beetle shell green/red/black, body brown, black ink
  • Silhouette: Round beetle, color-shifting shell, small legs
  • Lore: Truth Beetles are illegal in the Violet Highlands' Grand Assembly. Politicians find them inconvenient.

111. Breadcrumb Sprite A tiny creature that follows lost travelers, leaving small helpful clues: a pebble pointing the right way, a leaf marking safe water. No one knows who sends them. They never speak.

  • Palette: Brown, leaf green, pebble gray, soft glow, black ink
  • Silhouette: Tiny vague figure, leaving helpful objects
  • Lore: Breadcrumb Sprites are believed to be the Wanderer's doing, though he denies it. They have saved more lost dogs than any Order.

112. Weather Vane Bird A bird that always faces the direction the weather will come from tomorrow. Its feathers shift to match the predicted weather: gray for rain, gold for sun, white for snow.

  • Palette: Feather shifts by prediction, body brown, black ink
  • Silhouette: Bird, always facing one direction, feathers changing
  • Lore: Farmers and travelers keep Weather Vane Birds as pets. They are more accurate than any Deep prophecy for short-term prediction.

113. Story Spider A spider that webs stories instead of webs. The threads are invisible but if you run your paw through them, you experience a brief vivid memory that is not your own.

  • Palette: Web silver, story-color shifting, body black, black ink
  • Silhouette: Spider, invisible web threads, patient posture
  • Lore: Story Spiders are how the Deepwood preserves oral history. The Wild Order protects them fiercely. To destroy a Story Spider's web is to erase a memory.

114. Echo Egg An egg that hatches only when spoken to. What hatches depends on what is said. Kind words hatch gentle creatures. Cruel words hatch monsters. Most Echo Eggs are buried where they are found.

  • Palette: Egg white, echo ripple color, shell crack glow, black ink
  • Silhouette: Egg, faint glow, hairline cracks, waiting
  • Lore: Echo Eggs are rare and dangerous. The Dream Order specializes in hatching them safely, using only lullabies and soft promises.
