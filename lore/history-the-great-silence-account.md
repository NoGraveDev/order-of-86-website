## ACCOUNT 1: THE TIDEWATCH OFFICIAL LOG
*Written by Brother Deep-Current, Senior Poolkeeper, 1,100 YFH*

---

**Hour 0:**

The scrying pools went dark at dawn. Not cloudy. Not murky. Dark. As if someone had placed a lid over the water.

I checked the other pools. All dark. I checked the apprentices. All blind. Not physically blind — their eyes worked fine. But their inner sight was gone. They could not scry. They could not even feel the water.

I checked myself. I could feel the water. I could feel its temperature, its movement, its weight. But I could not feel its meaning. The water was just water.

**Hour 3:**

News arrived from the Forge Spire. Their fires had gone out. Not dwindled. Gone out. The central forge — which has burned continuously for 600 years — was cold. Highforge the Steady reported that he could not reignite it. Not with magic. Not with flint. The stone itself refused to burn.

**Hour 6:**

The Solar Spire's light-towers went dark. The Heartwood Spire's gardens stopped growing. The Violet Citadel's dimensional wards collapsed. The Dream Spire's sleepers woke screaming.

Only the Concordance Spire was unaffected. It has no magic. It is simply a building.

**Hour 12:**

The moons are still in the sky. I can see them. But they are not singing. They are not feeding the Current. They are simply... present. Like stones. Like the pre-Howl Silent Gods.

I have checked the astronomical records. The moons are in their correct positions. Their orbits have not changed. But their frequencies are gone.

**Hour 18:**

The first deaths.

A Radiant Wizard in the Violet Highlands was performing a purification ritual when the Silence began. She was holding a patient's infection in stasis. When the magic cut out, the infection returned instantly. She died in minutes.

A Flame Wizard in Frosthollow was maintaining a heating spell for a nursery. When the spell collapsed, the temperature dropped. Three pups froze before their mothers could warm them with body heat.

A Wild Wizard in the Deepwood was accelerating a crop's growth. When the magic stopped, the crop withered. The village that depended on it starved over the next month.

**Hour 24:**

Halfway.

The civilian population is panicking. Dogs who have never felt the Current are frightened by its absence. They describe it as "a hole where something used to be." Even unmagical dogs can feel the silence.

The Mute is celebrating. They are walking through the streets with broken hat brims around their necks, telling everyone that this is what freedom feels like.

I saw Ashpaw. He was standing on a rooftop, looking at the dark moons, not celebrating. Just looking. When he saw me, he said: "This is not what I wanted."

**Hour 36:**

The Loyals — dogs who are magically sensitive but not Wizards — are suffering the most. They feel the absence of the Current as a physical pain. Some have stopped eating. Some have stopped sleeping. Some have simply... stopped.

The Mute dogs are unaffected. They say this proves their point.

**Hour 42:**

I have been trying to scry the future of the Silence. I cannot. The pools are still dark. But I have been listening.

I hear something. Not a sound. An absence of sound. A frequency that is not there but should be. It is like a tooth that has been pulled. You feel the gap more than you felt the tooth.

I believe the Silence is not random. I believe it is patterned. The moons went dark in the order they woke: Emberhowl first, Palehowl last. This suggests coordination. This suggests intelligence.

I do not know whose intelligence.

**Hour 49:**

It ended.

Emberhowl blazed back to life first. Then Solaris. Then Deepwell. Then Evergreen. Then Umbra. Then Roseglow. Finally, Palehowl — faint at first, then steady.

The Current returned like a flood. Wizards who had been despairing suddenly had more power than they could hold. Accidents happened. Fires. Explosions. Dimensional rifts.

The Silence killed thousands. The return of magic killed hundreds more.

**Hour 50:**

I checked the astronomical records again. The moons' orbits have shifted slightly. Almost imperceptibly. But they have shifted.

I do not know what this means. I am recording it in case someone else does.

## ACCOUNT 2: THE FORGE SPIRE PERSONAL JOURNAL
*Written by Spark Ember-Young, apprentice Flame Wizard, 1,100 YFH*

---

I was asleep when it started.

I woke up cold. The Forge Spire is never cold. The furnaces burn day and night. But I was cold. I could see my breath.

I ran to the central forge. It was out. The fire was out. Highforge was standing there, staring at the cold stone, with a look on his face I have never seen before. He looked... small.

I tried to light it. I reached for my magic — the warmth that is always there, always waiting — and found nothing. Not emptiness. Nothing. Like reaching for a paw that has been amputated.

I screamed. I am not proud of it. But I screamed.

Highforge did not scream. He just sat down on the cold stone and put his head in his paws. He sat there for six hours. He did not move. He did not speak. He just sat.

When the magic came back, he stood up and said: "We will never speak of this again."

We spoke of it again.

## ACCOUNT 3: THE MUTE CAMP ORAL RECORDING
*Transcribed by Brother Tidewatch-Deep from a Mute elder's speech, 1,101 YFH*

---

[Howled] This is what Ashpaw told us the night the Silence ended.

He said: "The moons went dark. The magic died. And for forty-nine hours, we were dogs again. Simply dogs. Not Wizards. Not channels. Not servants of celestial fire. Just dogs.

"I walked through the Ember Wastes during the Silence. I saw a mother warming her pups with her body, not with a spell. I saw a pack hunting together, not with magical fire but with teeth and coordination. I saw dogs being loyal because they chose to be loyal, not because a moon told them to be.

"This is what I wanted. This is what I have been preaching. But I did not want it like this. I did not want dogs to freeze. I did not want pups to starve. I did not want the silence to be a thief.

"I wanted the silence to be a choice."

He was crying. I had never seen Ashpaw cry. He was the strongest dog I knew. But he was crying.

He said: "The Hollow did this. The Hollow reached up and grabbed the moons. And I do not know if the Hollow is our ally or our enemy. I do not know if the Hollow wants freedom or oblivion.

"I only know that forty-nine hours is too long. And not long enough."

## ACCOUNT 4: THE GRAY DOG'S STATEMENT
*Recorded by Sister Arcane-Theory, 1,100 YFH*

---

I found him on the unnamed mountain. He was sitting where he always sits. Looking at the moons.

I asked him: "What happened?"

He said: "The Hollow reached."

I asked: "Will it reach again?"

He said: "Yes."

I asked: "When?"

He said: "I do not know. The Hollow is patient. I am patient too. But I am older than patience. And the Hollow is older than me."

I asked: "Can we stop it?"

He looked at me. His glasses caught the light of the returning moons. He said: "You cannot stop the tide. You can only build higher walls. And even the highest wall will fall, if the tide is patient enough."

Then he turned back to the moons and would not speak again.

## ACCOUNT 5: THE CIVILIAN LETTERS
*Collected by the Scribepack, 1,100–1,101 YFH*

---

**Letter from a mother in the Violet Highlands:**

> My daughter was born during the Silence. She did not cry. She simply looked at me, with eyes that seemed to see everything and nothing. When the magic returned, she began to cry. She has not stopped crying since.

**Letter from a farmer in the Sunward Heights:**

> My crops died during the Silence. Not from cold — the Sunward Heights is always warm. They died from absence. It was as if the plants knew that something had left the world, and they did not want to live in a world without it.

**Letter from a hunter in the Deepwood:**

> I was tracking a deer when the Silence began. The deer stopped running. It simply stood there, looking up at the sky. I stood there too. We looked at each other for a long time. Then the deer walked away. I did not shoot it. I have not hunted since.

**Letter from a child in Frosthollow:**

> My brother is a Wizard. During the Silence, he could not make fire. He sat in the corner and shivered. I brought him blankets. I brought him food. I told him stories. I was the big sister for forty-nine hours. Now the magic is back and he is the Wizard again. I miss being the big sister.

## THE AFTERMATH

### The Unfinished Dead

Dogs who died during the Great Silence left no magical residue. Their hats did not return to the moons. Their dream-threads did not leave Echoes. Their bodies did not produce the usual oneiroic imprint.

Sister Deep-Current estimates that 4,000 dogs died during the Silence. Most from cold, hunger, or failed medical magic. Some from suicide: Wizards who could not bear the absence of their power.

These are the Unfinished Dead. And their absence is still felt.

### The Moon Shift

Brother Deep-Current's astronomical observation was correct. The moons' orbits shifted slightly during the Silence. The shift was approximately 0.3 degrees per moon.

The Arcane Order has calculated that, at the current rate of shift, the moons will be misaligned by approximately 5 degrees in 10,000 years. This is not an immediate threat. But it suggests that the Silence was not a simple on/off switch. It was a physical event that affected the moons' mass and momentum.

### The Mute's Growth

Before the Silence, the Mute had approximately 200 followers. After the Silence, they had 2,000. The growth was not due to theology. It was due to trauma.

Dogs who lost magic during the Silence and could not fully recover it — approximately 5% of all Wizards — joined the Mute not because they agreed with Ashpaw's doctrine but because they had nowhere else to go.

### The Orders' Response

Each Order responded differently:

- **Flame:** Highforge ordered all apprentices to learn non-magical fire-starting techniques. "A Wizard who cannot light a fire without magic," he said, "is not a Wizard. They are a beggar."
- **Radiant:** The Highseer established an emergency fund for civilian relief. It was the first time an Order had provided direct financial aid to non-Wizards.
- **Deep:** The Tidewatch began studying the Silence's pattern full-time. Their research budget tripled.
- **Wild:** The Heartwood tree produced a new root system designed to survive without magical enhancement. The Wild Order is planting these "Silence-roots" throughout Pawtheon.
- **Arcane:** The Violet Citadel classified all Silence research as top priority. They also began stockpiling non-magical weapons.
- **Dream:** The Dream Spire established a trauma counseling program for Wizards who experienced the Silence as a psychological wound.
